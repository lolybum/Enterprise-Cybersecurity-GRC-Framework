# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Enterprise Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery (BC/DR) Standard is to establish mandatory technical and operational requirements that ensure critical business processes and supporting technology services can continue during disruptive events and recover within approved recovery objectives.

This standard defines enterprise requirements for business continuity planning, disaster recovery planning, recovery strategies, testing, maintenance, and continuous improvement to strengthen organizational resilience.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to:

- Business units.
- Information Technology services.
- Cloud services.
- On-premises infrastructure.
- Enterprise applications.
- Data centers.
- Disaster Recovery sites.
- Network infrastructure.
- Identity services.
- Third-party providers supporting critical services.
- Employees and contractors with BC/DR responsibilities.

This standard applies to all critical business processes and supporting technology assets managed by or on behalf of the organization.

---

# 3. Objectives

The objectives of this standard are to:

- Ensure continuity of critical business operations.
- Minimize operational disruption.
- Support timely recovery of technology services.
- Meet Recovery Time Objectives (RTO).
- Meet Recovery Point Objectives (RPO).
- Strengthen operational resilience.
- Support regulatory compliance.
- Improve crisis preparedness.
- Reduce recovery risk.
- Promote continuous improvement.

---

# 4. Standard Statement

The organization shall establish, implement, maintain, and continuously improve Business Continuity (BC) and Disaster Recovery (DR) capabilities that support the continued delivery of critical business services during and after disruptive events.

Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery plans shall be documented, tested, maintained, and aligned with business priorities, regulatory requirements, and enterprise risk management objectives.

Recovery activities shall support the timely restoration of critical systems, applications, infrastructure, and business processes while minimizing the impact on customers, employees, and stakeholders.

---

# 5. Business Continuity Governance

The organization shall establish governance processes to ensure Business Continuity (BC) and Disaster Recovery (DR) capabilities are planned, implemented, maintained, and continuously improved.

Business Continuity governance requirements include:

- Documented Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery procedures.
- Defined governance roles and responsibilities.
- Executive oversight of BC/DR capabilities.
- Integration with Enterprise Risk Management (ERM).
- Coordination with Information Security and Information Technology.
- Alignment with Crisis Management procedures.
- Periodic review of BC/DR effectiveness.
- Continuous improvement of BC/DR capabilities.

Business Continuity governance shall be reviewed at least annually or following significant organizational, regulatory, technology, or business changes.

---

# 6. Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

A Business Impact Analysis (BIA) shall be conducted to identify critical business processes, supporting assets, and recovery priorities.

BIA requirements include:

- Identification of critical business functions.
- Identification of supporting applications.
- Identification of supporting infrastructure.
- Identification of business dependencies.
- Assessment of financial impact.
- Assessment of operational impact.
- Assessment of legal and regulatory impact.
- Assessment of reputational impact.
- Definition of Recovery Time Objectives (RTO).
- Definition of Recovery Point Objectives (RPO).

Business Impact Analyses shall be reviewed at least annually or following significant business changes.

---

# 7. Recovery Strategies

Recovery strategies shall be established for all critical business processes and supporting technology services.

Recovery strategy requirements include:

- Alternate processing capabilities.
- High availability architectures where required.
- Cloud recovery strategies.
- Disaster Recovery site utilization.
- Data backup integration.
- Redundant network connectivity.
- Workforce continuity planning.
- Vendor recovery coordination.
- Manual workarounds where appropriate.
- Recovery prioritization based on business criticality.

Recovery strategies shall be documented, approved, and periodically tested.

---

# 8. Recovery Time Objective (RTO)

Recovery Time Objectives (RTO) shall be established for all critical business services.

RTO requirements include:

- Alignment with Business Impact Analysis results.
- Approval by business owners.
- Documentation within Business Continuity Plans.
- Validation during recovery exercises.
- Periodic review.
- Risk-based adjustment where necessary.

Recovery capabilities shall support restoration of critical services within approved RTO targets.

---

# 9. Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

Recovery Point Objectives (RPO) shall be established to define the maximum acceptable amount of data loss following a disruptive event.

RPO requirements include:

- Alignment with Business Impact Analysis results.
- Approval by business owners.
- Documentation within Disaster Recovery Plans.
- Validation during disaster recovery testing.
- Review following significant business or technology changes.
- Integration with enterprise backup schedules.

Backup and recovery processes shall support approved RPO targets.

---

# 10. Disaster Recovery Planning

The organization shall maintain documented Disaster Recovery (DR) plans for all critical technology services.

Disaster Recovery plan requirements include:

- Recovery procedures for critical systems.
- Recovery prioritization.
- Infrastructure restoration procedures.
- Application recovery procedures.
- Database recovery procedures.
- Cloud service recovery procedures.
- Network recovery procedures.
- Contact information for key personnel.
- Recovery validation procedures.
- Plan maintenance and version control.

Disaster Recovery plans shall be readily available to authorized personnel during emergencies.
