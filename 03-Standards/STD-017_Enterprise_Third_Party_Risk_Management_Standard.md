# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Enterprise Third-Party Risk Management Standard is to establish mandatory security, governance, and operational requirements for identifying, assessing, onboarding, monitoring, and offboarding third-party service providers that access organizational information, systems, or facilities.

This standard ensures third-party relationships are managed using a consistent risk-based approach that protects organizational assets, supports regulatory compliance, and reduces supply chain cybersecurity risk.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to:

- Vendors.
- Suppliers.
- Contractors.
- Consultants.
- Managed Service Providers (MSPs).
- Managed Security Service Providers (MSSPs).
- Cloud Service Providers.
- Software vendors.
- Payment processors.
- Outsourcing providers.
- Professional service firms.
- Fourth-party providers where applicable.

This standard applies to all third parties that create, process, store, transmit, or have access to organizational information or technology assets.

---

# 3. Objectives

The objectives of this standard are to:

- Reduce third-party cyber risk.
- Standardize vendor security assessments.
- Strengthen supply chain security.
- Protect organizational information.
- Improve regulatory compliance.
- Support Zero Trust Architecture.
- Improve vendor oversight.
- Reduce operational risk.
- Improve cyber resilience.
- Support continuous vendor monitoring.

---

# 4. Standard Statement

The organization shall establish and maintain a risk-based Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) program that evaluates cybersecurity, privacy, operational, legal, and regulatory risks throughout the lifecycle of third-party relationships.

Third parties shall undergo security assessment before onboarding, continuous monitoring throughout the engagement, and secure offboarding upon contract termination.

Security requirements shall be incorporated into procurement, contracting, vendor management, and supplier oversight activities.
