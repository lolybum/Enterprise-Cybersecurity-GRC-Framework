---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Enterprise Security Awareness and Training Standard is to establish mandatory requirements for developing, delivering, and maintaining security awareness and role-based cybersecurity training across the organization.

This standard ensures that personnel understand their cybersecurity responsibilities, recognize cyber threats, protect organizational information, and respond appropriately to security incidents, thereby reducing organizational risk and strengthening cyber resilience.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to:

- Employees.
- Contractors.
- Temporary workers.
- Interns.
- Consultants.
- Third-party personnel with access to organizational systems.
- Executive leadership.
- Board members where applicable.
- Information Technology personnel.
- Information Security personnel.
- Privileged users.
- Developers.
- Cloud administrators.

This standard applies to all individuals who access organizational information systems or process organizational information.

---

# 3. Objectives

The objectives of this standard are to:

- Improve cybersecurity awareness.
- Reduce human-related cybersecurity risks.
- Strengthen phishing resistance.
- Promote secure handling of organizational information.
- Support regulatory compliance.
- Improve incident reporting.
- Reinforce Zero Trust principles.
- Reduce insider threats.
- Promote secure remote work practices.
- Establish a culture of cybersecurity.

---

# 4. Standard Statement

The organization shall establish, maintain, and continuously improve a Security Awareness and Training Program that provides personnel with the knowledge and skills required to protect organizational information and technology assets.

Security awareness activities shall be risk-based, role-specific where appropriate, regularly updated, and measured for effectiveness.

Training shall be mandatory for all personnel and completed within established organizational timeframes.
