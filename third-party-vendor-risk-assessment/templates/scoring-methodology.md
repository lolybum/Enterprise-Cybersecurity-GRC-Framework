# Vendor Risk Scoring Methodology

## Questionnaire Scoring

| Score | Definition |
|---:|---|
| 2 | Effective and supported by current, relevant evidence |
| 1 | Partially effective, incomplete, or supported by limited evidence |
| 0 | Ineffective, not implemented, or not evidenced |

`Control Effectiveness = Points Earned ÷ Available Points`

## Finding Risk

`Finding Risk = Likelihood × Impact`

### Likelihood

| Rating | Definition |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Rare |
| 2 | Unlikely |
| 3 | Possible |
| 4 | Likely |
| 5 | Almost certain |

### Impact

| Rating | Definition |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Insignificant |
| 2 | Minor |
| 3 | Moderate |
| 4 | Major |
| 5 | Severe |

### Risk Bands

| Score | Rating |
|---:|---|
| 20–25 | Critical |
| 12–19 | High |
| 6–11 | Moderate |
| 1–5 | Low |

## Overall Residual Risk

`Residual Risk = Inherent Risk × (1 − Control Effectiveness)`

Risk judgment may raise the calculated result when critical scenarios,
concentration, weak evidence, or risk-appetite concerns remain.
