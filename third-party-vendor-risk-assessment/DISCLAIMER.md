# Disclaimer

This repository is an educational cybersecurity GRC portfolio project.

CloudPay Solutions is a fictional company. Vendor responses, scores, evidence,
findings, risks, remediation actions, and approval decisions are simulated to
demonstrate a professional third-party risk assessment workflow.

The project does not contain real vendor confidential information, customer
data, credentials, proprietary reports, or legal advice. Organizations should
adapt the methodology, thresholds, and contractual requirements to their own
risk appetite, regulatory obligations, and legal guidance.
