# Executive Recommendation

## Decision

**Conditional Approval – High Residual Risk**

CloudPay Solutions demonstrates a generally mature security program supported
by current independent assurance, strong IAM, encryption, centralized
monitoring, secure development, and cyber-insurance coverage.

Unconditional approval is not recommended because the assessment identified
material gaps affecting incident notification, vulnerability remediation,
disaster recovery, fourth-party transparency, penetration-test assurance, data
deletion, and customer audit rights.

## Mandatory Contract Conditions

1. Confirmed security incidents must be reported within 24 hours.
2. The Data Processing Agreement must address confidentiality, processing
   purpose, deletion/return, audit cooperation, processing location, and
   subprocessors.
3. The vendor must provide a complete subprocessor register and at least 30
   days' advance notice of material changes.
4. The customer must retain reasonable assurance-review and incident-triggered
   audit rights.
5. Cyber-liability insurance of at least $10 million must remain in force.

## Mandatory Go-Live Conditions

1. Demonstrate a recovery capability that meets the 4-hour RTO and 1-hour RPO,
   or obtain a documented and approved business-continuity exception.
2. Provide detailed sanitized penetration-test results and verified closure of
   Critical and High findings.
3. Commit to remediating Critical vulnerabilities within 15 days and containing
   actively exploited vulnerabilities within 72 hours.
4. Validate SSO, MFA, least privilege, security logging, API controls, data
   minimization, retention, and deletion.

## Risk Acceptance

Any remaining High residual risk requires written acceptance by the business
executive and CISO, with concurrence from Legal and Privacy. Critical residual
risk must be escalated to the enterprise risk committee and may not be accepted
through the standard onboarding process.
