# GOV-046 Enterprise Artificial Intelligence (AI) Security and Governance Policy

**Document ID:** GOV-046

**Version:** 1.0

**Owner:** Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)

**Approved By:** Executive Management

**Effective Date:** July 2026

**Review Cycle:** Annual

**Classification:** Internal

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Enterprise Artificial Intelligence (AI) Security and Governance Policy is to establish governance requirements for the secure, responsible, ethical, and compliant development, acquisition, deployment, operation, and use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Generative AI technologies across the organization.

This policy establishes a standardized enterprise AI governance framework that manages AI-related risks, protects organizational information, promotes trustworthy AI, supports regulatory compliance, and enables the responsible adoption of AI technologies while protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of enterprise information assets.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All employees.
- Contractors.
- Consultants.
- Temporary workers.
- Interns.
- Executive leadership.
- Information Technology personnel.
- Information Security personnel.
- Data Science teams.
- Software Development teams.
- Business units implementing AI solutions.
- Third-party AI service providers.
- Cloud-hosted AI platforms.
- Vendors providing AI-enabled products or services.

This policy applies to all Artificial Intelligence technologies used by or on behalf of the organization, including:

- Machine Learning (ML) systems.
- Generative AI platforms.
- Large Language Models (LLMs).
- AI-powered chatbots.
- Predictive analytics platforms.
- AI-assisted software development tools.
- AI-enabled security technologies.
- AI APIs and cloud AI services.
- Autonomous decision-support systems.
- AI models developed internally or acquired from third parties.

---

# 3. Objectives

The objectives of this policy are to:

- Establish enterprise AI governance.
- Promote responsible and trustworthy AI.
- Protect organizational information used by AI systems.
- Reduce AI-related cybersecurity and privacy risks.
- Support ethical AI practices.
- Ensure human oversight of high-risk AI decisions.
- Strengthen AI model security.
- Improve AI risk management.
- Enable secure adoption of AI technologies.
- Support legal, regulatory, and contractual compliance.

---

# 4. Policy Statement

The organization shall maintain a formal Enterprise Artificial Intelligence (AI) Security and Governance Program governing the evaluation, approval, implementation, operation, monitoring, and retirement of AI technologies.

AI systems shall be deployed only after appropriate governance, security, privacy, legal, compliance, and risk management reviews have been completed.

The use of AI technologies shall protect organizational information, respect privacy requirements, support transparency, enable human oversight where appropriate, and comply with applicable laws, regulations, contractual obligations, and enterprise policies.

The organization shall continuously monitor AI technologies for security risks, model performance, data protection, regulatory compliance, and emerging AI threats throughout the AI system lifecycle.

---

# 5. AI Governance

The organization shall maintain a formal governance framework for the secure, ethical, and responsible use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies.

AI governance shall include:

- Executive sponsorship.
- Defined governance roles and responsibilities.
- AI governance standards.
- Risk-based AI decision-making.
- Regulatory compliance oversight.
- AI ethics oversight.
- AI performance monitoring.
- Executive reporting.
- Periodic governance reviews.
- Continuous improvement initiatives.

AI governance shall align with Enterprise Risk Management, Information Security, Privacy, Legal, Compliance, and Data Governance functions.

---

# 6. AI Risk Management

The organization shall identify, assess, manage, and monitor risks associated with Artificial Intelligence systems throughout their lifecycle.

AI risk management activities include:

- AI risk assessments.
- Security risk assessments.
- Privacy impact assessments.
- Legal and regulatory reviews.
- Third-party risk assessments.
- Model security reviews.
- Data quality assessments.
- Operational risk assessments.
- Bias assessments.
- Periodic risk reassessments.

AI risks shall be documented and managed within the Enterprise Risk Management Program.

---

# 7. AI System Classification

Artificial Intelligence systems shall be classified according to their business purpose, risk, and potential organizational impact.

Classification categories may include:

- Low-risk AI systems.
- Moderate-risk AI systems.
- High-risk AI systems.
- Safety-critical AI systems.
- Customer-facing AI systems.
- Internal business AI systems.
- Security AI systems.
- Decision-support AI systems.
- Autonomous AI systems.
- Experimental AI systems.

Security controls shall be implemented based on the assigned risk classification.

---

# 8. Approved AI Services

Only approved Artificial Intelligence services shall be used for organizational business activities.

Approval requirements include:

- Business justification.
- Security assessment.
- Privacy review.
- Legal review.
- Vendor due diligence.
- Contract review.
- Data protection assessment.
- AI risk assessment.
- Executive approval where required.
- Documentation of approval.

The use of unapproved AI services for organizational information is prohibited.

---

# 9. Generative AI Usage

Generative AI technologies shall be used responsibly and in accordance with enterprise governance requirements.

Generative AI usage requirements include:

- Use only approved Generative AI platforms.
- Do not submit confidential or regulated information to unauthorized AI services.
- Validate AI-generated content before business use.
- Protect intellectual property.
- Comply with copyright requirements.
- Comply with privacy requirements.
- Obtain approval for high-risk AI use cases.
- Clearly identify AI-generated content where required.
- Report suspected misuse of AI technologies.
- Follow enterprise AI governance standards.

Human review shall be required before AI-generated outputs are used for critical business decisions.

---

# 10. AI Data Protection

Information processed by Artificial Intelligence systems shall be protected according to enterprise information security requirements.

AI data protection requirements include:

- Data classification.
- Data minimization.
- Encryption of sensitive information.
- Access control enforcement.
- Secure data storage.
- Secure data transmission.
- Data retention controls.
- Secure data disposal.
- Audit logging.
- Regulatory compliance.

AI systems shall process only the minimum information necessary to achieve authorized business objectives.

---

# 11. AI Model Security

Artificial Intelligence models shall be protected throughout their lifecycle against unauthorized access, manipulation, theft, misuse, and compromise.

AI model security requirements include:

- Secure model development.
- Secure model storage.
- Model integrity verification.
- Access control enforcement.
- Protection against model tampering.
- Protection against model theft.
- Secure model deployment.
- Secure model updates.
- Vulnerability assessments.
- Continuous security monitoring.

AI models shall undergo security testing before deployment into production environments.

---

# 12. Third-Party AI Services

Third-party Artificial Intelligence providers shall be evaluated before organizational use.

Third-party AI requirements include:

- Vendor due diligence.
- Security assessments.
- Privacy assessments.
- AI risk assessments.
- Contractual security obligations.
- Regulatory compliance verification.
- Data processing agreement review.
- Service monitoring.
- Periodic vendor reassessment.
- Incident notification requirements.

Third-party AI services shall comply with the Enterprise Third-Party Security Policy.

---

# 13. Responsible AI Principles

The organization shall promote responsible and trustworthy Artificial Intelligence practices.

Responsible AI principles include:

- Fairness.
- Transparency.
- Accountability.
- Explainability.
- Human oversight.
- Privacy protection.
- Security by design.
- Reliability.
- Robustness.
- Regulatory compliance.

Responsible AI principles shall be incorporated throughout the AI lifecycle.

---

# 14. Human Oversight

Appropriate human oversight shall be maintained for Artificial Intelligence systems, particularly those supporting high-risk business activities.

Human oversight requirements include:

- Human review of high-risk AI decisions.
- Executive approval where appropriate.
- Escalation of AI anomalies.
- Validation of AI-generated recommendations.
- Manual override capability.
- Periodic review of AI decisions.
- Monitoring for bias.
- Monitoring for unintended outcomes.
- Documentation of oversight activities.
- Continuous improvement.

AI shall support—not replace—human judgment for critical business, legal, regulatory, financial, healthcare, or security decisions.

---

# 15. AI Monitoring and Auditing

Artificial Intelligence systems shall be continuously monitored to ensure security, performance, compliance, and reliability.

Monitoring activities include:

- AI performance monitoring.
- Security event monitoring.
- Model drift detection.
- Bias monitoring.
- Accuracy validation.
- Audit logging.
- Access monitoring.
- Regulatory compliance monitoring.
- Incident reporting.
- Periodic AI audits.

Monitoring results shall be reviewed regularly to identify improvement opportunities.

# 16. Roles and Responsibilities

## Executive Management

Executive Management shall:

- Approve the Enterprise AI Security and Governance Program.
- Allocate appropriate resources.
- Review enterprise AI risks.
- Support responsible AI initiatives.

## Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)

The CISO shall:

- Own this policy.
- Oversee AI security governance.
- Approve AI security standards.
- Review AI security metrics.
- Report significant AI risks to Executive Management.

## AI Governance Committee

The AI Governance Committee shall:

- Review proposed AI use cases.
- Approve high-risk AI implementations.
- Monitor responsible AI practices.
- Review AI risk assessments.
- Recommend governance improvements.
- Oversee AI compliance activities.

## Information Security Team

The Information Security Team shall:

- Assess AI security risks.
- Monitor AI security controls.
- Investigate AI-related security incidents.
- Conduct AI security assessments.
- Recommend security improvements.
- Maintain AI security documentation.

## Information Technology Team

The Information Technology Team shall:

- Deploy approved AI technologies.
- Maintain AI infrastructure.
- Implement security controls.
- Monitor system availability.
- Support AI integrations.
- Maintain technical documentation.

## Business Owners

Business Owners shall:

- Define business requirements for AI solutions.
- Ensure AI use aligns with organizational objectives.
- Participate in AI risk assessments.
- Validate AI outputs.
- Report AI-related concerns.

## Users

Users shall:

- Use only approved AI services.
- Protect organizational information.
- Validate AI-generated outputs before business use.
- Report suspected AI misuse or security incidents.
- Comply with this policy and related standards.

# 17. Compliance

Compliance with this policy shall be verified through:

- Internal audits.
- External audits.
- AI governance assessments.
- AI risk management reviews.
- AI security assessments.
- Privacy compliance reviews.
- AI model security testing.

# 18. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy shall:

- Be formally documented.
- Include business justification.
- Include a documented risk assessment.
- Identify compensating security controls.
- Be approved by the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO).
- Include an expiration date.
- Be reviewed annually.

Approved exceptions shall be maintained within the Enterprise Exception Register.

# 22. Definitions

**Artificial Intelligence (AI)** – Computer systems designed to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, including learning, reasoning, prediction, content generation, and decision support.

**Generative AI** – Artificial Intelligence capable of creating text, images, code, audio, video, or other content based on user prompts or input data.

**Large Language Model (LLM)** – A machine learning model trained on large datasets to understand and generate human language.

**AI Risk Management** – The process of identifying, assessing, mitigating, monitoring, and communicating risks associated with Artificial Intelligence systems throughout their lifecycle.

**Model Drift** – The degradation of an AI model's performance over time due to changes in data, environments, or user behavior.

**Responsible AI** – The design, development, deployment, and operation of Artificial Intelligence systems in a manner that is ethical, secure, transparent, accountable, and compliant with applicable laws and organizational policies.
