# GOV-008 Enterprise Information Classification Policy

**Document ID:** GOV-008
**Version:** 1.0
**Owner:** Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)
**Approved By:** Executive Management
**Effective Date:** July 2026
**Review Cycle:** Annual

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish enterprise requirements for classifying information according to its sensitivity, business value, and regulatory requirements. Proper classification ensures that information receives appropriate protection throughout its lifecycle.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All employees
- Contractors
- Third-party service providers
- Business units
- Information assets
- Electronic information
- Physical documents
- Cloud services
- Email communications
- Databases
- Backup media

---

# 3. Objectives

The organization shall:

- Classify information consistently.
- Protect sensitive information.
- Support regulatory compliance.
- Reduce information disclosure risks.
- Improve data governance.
- Support business operations.
- Protect customer information.

---

# 4. Policy Statement

All organizational information shall be classified according to its sensitivity and business value before it is created, stored, processed, transmitted, or disposed of.

Information classification shall determine:

- Access permissions
- Encryption requirements
- Storage requirements
- Transmission methods
- Retention periods
- Disposal requirements

---

# 5. Information Classification Levels

Enterprise information shall be classified as:

- Public
- Internal
- Confidential
- Restricted

Each classification level shall include appropriate handling and protection requirements.

---

# 6. Information Ownership

Every information asset shall have an assigned Information Owner responsible for:

- Information classification
- Access authorization
- Periodic review
- Regulatory compliance
- Risk acceptance where applicable
- Retention requirements
- Secure disposal approval

Information Owners shall review classifications annually or whenever business requirements change.

---

# 7. Classification Criteria

Information shall be classified based on:

- Business value
- Confidentiality requirements
- Integrity requirements
- Availability requirements
- Legal obligations
- Regulatory requirements
- Contractual obligations
- Privacy requirements

Classification decisions shall be documented where appropriate.

---

# 8. Public Information

Public information:

- May be disclosed without restriction.
- Requires integrity protection.
- Shall be approved before publication.
- May be shared externally.
- Shall remain accurate and current.

Examples include:

- Public website content
- Marketing materials
- Published reports
- Press releases

---

# 9. Internal Information

Internal information:

- Shall be accessible only to authorized personnel.
- Shall not be publicly disclosed without approval.
- May be shared internally on a need-to-know basis.
- Shall be protected from unauthorized modification.

Examples include:

- Internal procedures
- Department documentation
- Internal emails
- Organization charts

---

# 10. Confidential Information

Confidential information:

- Shall be protected using appropriate security controls.
- Shall be encrypted where appropriate.
- Shall only be accessed by authorized personnel.
- Shall require business justification for access.
- Shall not be transmitted through unauthorized channels.

Examples include:

- Customer information
- Financial records
- Employee information
- Business contracts
- Security documentation
- Internal audit reports

---

# 11. Restricted Information

Restricted information represents the organization's most sensitive information.

Restricted information:

- Shall only be accessed by explicitly authorized personnel.
- Shall require Executive Management approval where applicable.
- Shall always be encrypted during storage and transmission.
- Shall be logged and monitored.
- Shall have the highest level of protection.

Examples include:

- Cryptographic keys
- Authentication credentials
- Security incident investigations
- Regulatory investigations
- Merger and acquisition documents
- Highly sensitive intellectual property

---

# 12. Information Handling Requirements

Information handling requirements shall include:

- Proper classification
- Secure storage
- Authorized access
- Encryption where required
- Secure transmission
- Secure backup
- Proper retention
- Secure disposal

Handling requirements shall be applied throughout the information lifecycle.

---

# 13. Information Labeling

Information assets shall be clearly labeled according to their classification.

Labels shall appear where appropriate on:

- Electronic documents
- Printed documents
- Email messages
- Shared folders
- Database records
- Backup media

Automated labeling technologies should be used whenever practical.

---

# 14. Storage and Transmission

Information shall be stored and transmitted according to its classification.

Requirements include:

- Approved storage locations
- Encryption for sensitive information
- Secure file transfer methods
- Approved cloud storage services
- Secure email solutions
- Access logging
- Backup protection

Unauthorized storage or transmission methods are prohibited.

---

# 15. Access Management

Access to classified information shall:

- Follow least privilege principles.
- Require business justification.
- Be approved by the Information Owner.
- Be periodically reviewed.
- Be immediately revoked upon termination or role change.
- Be monitored for unauthorized activity.

Privileged access shall require enhanced monitoring and approval.

---

# 16. Compliance

Compliance with this policy shall be monitored through:

- Internal audits
- External audits
- Information classification reviews
- Access reviews
- Regulatory assessments
- Management reviews

This policy supports compliance with:

- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022
- CIS Controls v8
- COBIT 2019
- Applicable legal and regulatory requirements

---

# 17. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy shall:

- Be documented.
- Include business justification.
- Be approved by Executive Management.
- Include compensating controls.
- Include an expiration date.
- Be reviewed periodically.

All approved exceptions shall be maintained within the enterprise exception register.

# 20. Definitions

**Information Classification** – The process of assigning sensitivity levels to information based on its value, legal requirements, and business impact.

**Information Owner** – Individual responsible for determining the classification and protection requirements of information.

**Confidential Information** – Information that could cause significant harm if disclosed without authorization.

**Restricted Information** – The organization's most sensitive information requiring the highest level of protection.
