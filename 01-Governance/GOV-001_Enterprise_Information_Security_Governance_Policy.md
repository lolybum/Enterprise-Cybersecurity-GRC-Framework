# GOV-001 Enterprise Information Security Governance Policy

**Document ID:** GOV-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)  
**Approved By:** Executive Leadership Team  
**Effective Date:** July 2026  
**Review Cycle:** Annual  
**Classification:** Internal Use

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish the governance framework for managing information security across the enterprise. This policy defines executive accountability, governance responsibilities, decision-making authority, and oversight mechanisms required to protect organizational information assets while supporting business objectives.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- Employees
- Contractors
- Consultants
- Third-party vendors
- Temporary staff
- Business partners
- Cloud service providers

It covers all information systems, applications, networks, cloud environments, endpoints, and data owned or managed by the organization.

---

# 3. Governance Objectives

The organization shall establish an information security governance program that:

- Aligns cybersecurity with business strategy.
- Protects confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information.
- Supports regulatory compliance.
- Enables risk-based decision making.
- Promotes continuous improvement.
- Defines executive accountability.

---

# 4. Governance Principles

The organization adopts the following principles:

- Risk-Based Security
- Defense in Depth
- Least Privilege
- Zero Trust
- Continuous Monitoring
- Regulatory Compliance
- Executive Accountability
- Continuous Improvement
