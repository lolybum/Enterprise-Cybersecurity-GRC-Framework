# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Enterprise Backup and Recovery Policy is to establish governance requirements for the backup, protection, restoration, and recovery of organizational information systems, applications, and data to ensure business continuity, operational resilience, and rapid recovery from cyber incidents, system failures, natural disasters, and other disruptive events.

This policy establishes a standardized enterprise framework for backup and recovery processes that protects the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of organizational information while supporting legal, contractual, and regulatory obligations.

---

This policy applies to:

- All employees.
- Contractors.
- Consultants.
- Temporary workers.
- Third-party vendors.
- Managed service providers.
- Cloud environments.
- On-premises environments.
- Hybrid environments.
- Servers.
- Endpoints.
- Databases.
- Applications.
- Email systems.
- Virtual machines.
- Containers.
- Backup repositories.
- Network-attached storage.
- Removable backup media.

This policy applies to all enterprise systems and information assets that require backup and recovery capabilities.

---

# 3. Objectives

The objectives of this policy are to:

- Ensure reliable backup of enterprise information.
- Support rapid restoration of critical services.
- Protect backup data against loss, corruption, ransomware, and unauthorized access.
- Establish Recovery Time Objectives (RTOs) and Recovery Point Objectives (RPOs).
- Improve operational resilience.
- Support business continuity and disaster recovery.
- Ensure regulatory and contractual compliance.
- Protect the integrity and availability of enterprise data.
- Standardize backup governance across the organization.

---

# 4. Policy Statement

The organization shall maintain a formal Enterprise Backup and Recovery Program that governs the creation, protection, storage, testing, restoration, and monitoring of backups for enterprise information systems and data.

Backup and recovery activities shall be risk-based, documented, regularly tested, and integrated with Business Continuity, Disaster Recovery, Incident Management, and Information Security governance processes to ensure timely recovery from disruptive events.

---

# 5. Backup Requirements

The organization shall implement backup processes that protect enterprise information against accidental deletion, hardware failure, cyberattacks, ransomware, corruption, and other disruptive events.

Backup requirements include:

- Identification of critical systems.
- Identification of critical data.
- Risk-based backup schedules.
- Automated backup processes where feasible.
- Backup integrity verification.
- Backup monitoring.
- Backup failure notification.
- Secure backup documentation.
- Periodic backup reviews.
- Compliance with enterprise backup standards.

All production systems shall have documented backup requirements approved by the appropriate Data Owner and System Owner.

---

# 6. Backup Types

The Enterprise Backup and Recovery Program shall support multiple backup methods based on business requirements.

Supported backup types include:

- Full backups.
- Incremental backups.
- Differential backups.
- Snapshot backups.
- Image-based backups.
- Database backups.
- Application backups.
- Virtual machine backups.
- Cloud-native backups.
- Immutable backups where supported.

Backup strategies shall be selected according to business impact, Recovery Time Objectives (RTOs), and Recovery Point Objectives (RPOs).

---

# 7. Recovery Objectives (RTO/RPO)

Recovery objectives shall be established for all critical business systems.

Recovery planning shall include:

- Recovery Time Objective (RTO).
- Recovery Point Objective (RPO).
- Maximum Tolerable Downtime (MTD).
- Business Impact Analysis (BIA).
- Recovery prioritization.
- Service restoration sequence.
- Critical dependency mapping.
- Resource availability.
- Recovery validation.
- Executive approval for critical recovery objectives.

Recovery objectives shall be reviewed annually and after significant business or technology changes.

---

# 8. Backup Encryption

Backup data shall be protected using approved cryptographic controls.

Encryption requirements include:

- Encryption of Confidential information.
- Encryption of Restricted information.
- Encryption of backup repositories.
- Encryption during transmission.
- Secure cryptographic key management.
- Access-controlled encryption keys.
- Compliance with enterprise encryption standards.
- Secure cloud backup encryption.
- Encryption monitoring.
- Periodic encryption validation.

Encryption technologies shall comply with the Enterprise Encryption and Cryptographic Key Management Policy.

---

# 9. Backup Storage

Backup data shall be stored using approved enterprise storage solutions.

Storage requirements include:

- On-site backup storage.
- Off-site backup storage.
- Cloud backup repositories.
- Immutable backup storage where supported.
- Geographic redundancy.
- Physical security.
- Environmental protection.
- Access controls.
- Storage capacity monitoring.
- Disaster recovery integration.

Backup repositories shall be protected against unauthorized access, ransomware, and physical damage.

---

# 10. Backup Retention

Backup copies shall be retained according to documented retention schedules and business requirements.

Retention requirements include:

- Daily backup retention.
- Weekly backup retention.
- Monthly backup retention.
- Annual archival backups.
- Regulatory retention requirements.
- Legal hold support.
- Secure deletion after expiration.
- Periodic retention reviews.
- Documentation of retention schedules.
- Compliance monitoring.

Backup retention periods shall align with the Enterprise Data Retention and Secure Disposal Policy.

# 22. Definitions

**Backup** – A copy of data, applications, or system configurations created to enable restoration following loss, corruption, or disruption.

**Recovery** – The process of restoring systems, applications, or data to normal operational status following an incident or outage.

**Recovery Time Objective (RTO)** – The maximum acceptable amount of time required to restore a system or service after an interruption.

**Recovery Point Objective (RPO)** – The maximum acceptable amount of data loss measured in time before an interruption.

**Immutable Backup** – A backup that cannot be modified, encrypted, or deleted during its defined retention period.

**Business Impact Analysis (BIA)** – A process used to identify critical business functions and determine recovery priorities and objectives.
