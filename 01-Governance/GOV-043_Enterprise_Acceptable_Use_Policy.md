# GOV-043 Enterprise Acceptable Use Policy

**Document ID:** GOV-043

**Owner:** Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)

**Approved By:** Executive Management

**Effective Date:** July 2026

**Review Cycle:** Annual

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Enterprise Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) is to establish governance requirements for the appropriate, secure, ethical, and responsible use of organizational information systems, networks, devices, applications, cloud services, artificial intelligence (AI) technologies, internet resources, and other technology assets.

This policy establishes a standardized enterprise framework that defines acceptable and prohibited use of organizational technology resources to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of organizational information, reduce cybersecurity risk, support regulatory compliance, and promote responsible user behavior.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All employees.
- Contractors.
- Consultants.
- Temporary workers.
- Interns.
- Third-party vendors.
- Managed service providers.
- Business partners with authorized access.
- Remote workers.
- Executive leadership.
- Information Technology personnel.
- Information Security personnel.
- Cloud service users.
- Mobile device users.
- Users of organizational information systems.

This policy applies to all organizational technology resources, including:

- Corporate-owned devices.
- Bring Your Own Device (BYOD) environments.
- Networks.
- Servers.
- Endpoints.
- Cloud services.
- Applications.
- Email systems.
- Collaboration platforms.
- Artificial Intelligence (AI) services.
- Internet services.
- Organizational data and information assets.

---

# 3. Objectives

The objectives of this policy are to:

- Establish enterprise acceptable use requirements.
- Protect organizational information assets.
- Promote responsible and ethical technology use.
- Reduce cybersecurity risks caused by user behavior.
- Support regulatory, legal, and contractual compliance.
- Define prohibited technology activities.
- Protect organizational reputation.
- Support secure remote work.
- Promote responsible use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies.
- Strengthen enterprise cybersecurity governance.

---

# 4. Policy Statement

The organization shall maintain a formal Enterprise Acceptable Use Program governing the use of organizational technology resources.

Users shall access and use organizational systems, networks, applications, cloud services, devices, and information only for authorized business purposes unless limited personal use is explicitly permitted by organizational policy.

All users are responsible for protecting organizational information, complying with enterprise security policies, safeguarding authentication credentials, reporting suspected security incidents, and using technology resources in a secure, lawful, ethical, and responsible manner.

Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action, suspension of access privileges, termination of employment or contractual relationships, and legal or regulatory action where applicable.

---

# 5. Acceptable Use Governance

The organization shall maintain a formal governance framework to oversee the acceptable use of enterprise technology resources.

Acceptable use governance shall include:

- Executive sponsorship.
- Defined governance roles and responsibilities.
- Enterprise acceptable use standards.
- Risk-based policy management.
- Regulatory compliance oversight.
- User awareness and education.
- Compliance monitoring.
- Periodic policy reviews.
- Executive reporting.
- Continuous improvement initiatives.

Acceptable use governance shall align with Enterprise Information Security, Risk Management, Human Resources, and Compliance functions.

---

# 6. User Responsibilities

All users of organizational technology resources shall:

- Use organizational resources responsibly.
- Protect authentication credentials.
- Secure organizational devices.
- Follow enterprise security policies.
- Report suspected security incidents immediately.
- Protect confidential and sensitive information.
- Lock unattended devices.
- Install only authorized software.
- Complete required security awareness training.
- Cooperate with security investigations when required.

Users are personally responsible for activities performed using their assigned accounts.

---

# 7. Authorized Use

Organizational technology resources shall be used primarily for legitimate business purposes.

Authorized use includes:

- Business communications.
- Approved collaboration activities.
- Customer support.
- Business research.
- Authorized cloud services.
- Approved software usage.
- Remote work activities.
- Security administration.
- Business continuity activities.
- Limited personal use where permitted by organizational policy.

All authorized use shall comply with organizational policies and applicable laws.

---

# 8. Prohibited Activities

Users shall not engage in activities that compromise organizational security or violate legal, regulatory, or contractual obligations.

Prohibited activities include:

- Unauthorized access to systems or data.
- Password sharing.
- Installation of unauthorized software.
- Circumvention of security controls.
- Unauthorized vulnerability scanning.
- Unauthorized penetration testing.
- Downloading malicious software.
- Illegal or unethical activities.
- Unauthorized disclosure of confidential information.
- Misuse of organizational technology resources.

Violations may result in disciplinary action and legal consequences.

---

# 9. Internet Usage

Internet access shall be used responsibly and in support of legitimate business activities.

Internet usage requirements include:

- Access only appropriate websites.
- Avoid high-risk or malicious websites.
- Do not bypass web filtering controls.
- Do not download unauthorized software.
- Exercise caution when downloading files.
- Protect organizational information while browsing.
- Report suspicious websites.
- Use approved web browsers.
- Follow organizational internet security standards.
- Comply with applicable laws and regulations.

Internet activity may be monitored to protect organizational resources.

---

# 10. Email and Messaging Use

Organizational email and messaging platforms shall be used securely and responsibly.

Email and messaging requirements include:

- Use approved communication platforms.
- Verify recipients before sending sensitive information.
- Do not open suspicious attachments or links.
- Report phishing attempts immediately.
- Protect confidential information.
- Use encryption where required.
- Follow records retention requirements.
- Avoid sending offensive or inappropriate content.
- Comply with enterprise communication standards.
- Support regulatory and legal obligations.

Email communications are organizational records and may be monitored, retained, and reviewed in accordance with applicable policies and legal requirements.

---

# 11. Mobile Device Use

Organizational and authorized personal mobile devices used to access enterprise resources shall comply with enterprise security requirements.

Mobile device requirements include:

- Device registration where required.
- Screen lock protection.
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- Device encryption.
- Automatic security updates.
- Approved mobile applications.
- Remote wipe capability where applicable.
- Immediate reporting of lost or stolen devices.
- Secure wireless connectivity.
- Compliance with Mobile Device Management (MDM) requirements where implemented.

Mobile devices shall not be used in a manner that compromises organizational information.

---

# 12. Remote Work

Personnel authorized to work remotely shall protect organizational information and technology resources.

Remote work requirements include:

- Use approved remote access solutions.
- Connect through secure VPN services where required.
- Protect home networks using appropriate security controls.
- Prevent unauthorized viewing of sensitive information.
- Lock devices when unattended.
- Store organizational information securely.
- Use only approved collaboration tools.
- Report suspected security incidents immediately.
- Comply with enterprise security policies.
- Participate in required remote work security training.

Remote work activities shall comply with the Enterprise Remote Access Policy.

---

# 13. Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Generative AI Usage

The use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Generative AI services shall be governed to protect organizational information and intellectual property.

AI usage requirements include:

- Use only approved AI services for business purposes.
- Do not submit confidential, regulated, or proprietary information into unauthorized AI platforms.
- Verify AI-generated content for accuracy before use.
- Protect intellectual property and copyrighted material.
- Comply with privacy, legal, and regulatory requirements.
- Obtain approval before using AI tools for high-risk business processes.
- Clearly identify AI-generated content where required.
- Report suspected misuse of AI technologies.
- Follow enterprise AI governance standards.
- Participate in AI awareness training.

AI technologies shall be used responsibly and shall not replace required human review for critical business decisions.

---

# 14. Social Media Usage

Personnel shall use social media responsibly when representing or referencing the organization.

Social media requirements include:

- Protect confidential information.
- Do not disclose sensitive business information.
- Do not represent personal opinions as official organizational positions.
- Follow organizational branding guidelines.
- Respect intellectual property rights.
- Comply with legal and regulatory obligations.
- Report impersonation or fraudulent accounts.
- Maintain professional conduct.
- Avoid sharing security-sensitive information.
- Follow enterprise communication policies.

Official organizational communications shall be made only by authorized personnel.

---

# 15. Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)

Where permitted, personally owned devices used for business purposes shall comply with enterprise security requirements.

BYOD requirements include:

- Device registration.
- Security configuration verification.
- Device encryption.
- Screen lock protection.
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- Approved antivirus or endpoint protection.
- Operating system updates.
- Remote wipe capability where applicable.
- Separation of personal and organizational data where supported.
- Compliance with enterprise BYOD standards.

The organization reserves the right to restrict or revoke BYOD access if security requirements are not met.

# 16. Roles and Responsibilities

## Executive Management

Executive Management shall:

- Approve the Enterprise Acceptable Use Program.
- Allocate appropriate resources.
- Promote responsible technology use.
- Review compliance metrics.

## Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)

The CISO shall:

- Own this policy.
- Oversee acceptable use governance.
- Approve acceptable use standards.
- Review policy effectiveness.
- Report significant acceptable use risks to Executive Management.

## Information Security Team

The Information Security Team shall:

- Develop acceptable use standards.
- Monitor policy compliance.
- Investigate policy violations.
- Support awareness initiatives.
- Recommend security improvements.
- Maintain policy documentation.

## Information Technology Team

The Information Technology Team shall:

- Implement technical controls.
- Manage user access.
- Support endpoint security.
- Monitor organizational technology resources.
- Maintain enterprise systems.
- Support compliance activities.

## Human Resources

Human Resources shall:

- Communicate acceptable use requirements.
- Coordinate policy acknowledgments.
- Support disciplinary processes.
- Maintain personnel records related to policy compliance.
- Coordinate awareness training.

## Employees

Employees shall:

- Comply with this policy.
- Protect organizational information.
- Report security incidents.
- Use organizational technology responsibly.
- Complete required security awareness training.

# 18. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy shall:

- Be formally documented.
- Include business justification.
- Include a documented risk assessment.
- Identify compensating security controls.
- Be approved by the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO).
- Include an expiration date.
- Be reviewed annually.

Approved exceptions shall be maintained within the Enterprise Exception Register.

# 22. Definitions

**Acceptable Use** – The authorized, ethical, secure, and responsible use of organizational technology resources in accordance with enterprise policies and applicable laws.

**Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)** – A program that permits authorized personnel to use personally owned devices to access organizational systems and information.

**Remote Work** – The performance of authorized business activities from locations outside organizational facilities using approved technologies.

**Artificial Intelligence (AI)** – Computer systems capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence, including Generative AI technologies.

**Generative AI** – Artificial intelligence capable of creating text, images, code, audio, video, or other content based on user prompts or input.

**Organizational Technology Resources** – All systems, networks, devices, applications, cloud services, communication platforms, and information assets owned, leased, or managed by the organization.
