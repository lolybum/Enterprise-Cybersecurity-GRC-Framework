# GOV-009 Enterprise Access Control Policy

**Document ID:** GOV-009  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)  
**Approved By:** Executive Management  
**Effective Date:** July 2026  
**Review Cycle:** Annual

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish enterprise requirements for granting, managing, reviewing, and revoking access to information systems, applications, networks, and data. Proper access control ensures that only authorized individuals have access to organizational resources based on business need.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All employees
- Contractors
- Third-party service providers
- Temporary staff
- Consultants
- All information systems
- Cloud services
- On-premises infrastructure
- Mobile devices
- Business applications

---

# 3. Objectives

The organization shall:

- Protect information assets.
- Enforce least privilege.
- Support Zero Trust principles.
- Prevent unauthorized access.
- Reduce insider threats.
- Meet regulatory requirements.
- Maintain accountability.

---

# 4. Policy Statement

Access to organizational information systems shall be granted only after proper authorization and business justification.

All access shall:

- Be approved before provisioning.
- Follow least privilege.
- Be role-based where possible.
- Be periodically reviewed.
- Be revoked immediately when no longer required.

---

# 5. Access Control Principles

Access management shall follow:

- Least Privilege
- Need-to-Know
- Separation of Duties
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Zero Trust Security

---

# 6. User Account Management

User accounts shall:

- Be uniquely assigned.
- Be linked to an individual.
- Require management approval.
- Be disabled upon termination.
- Be periodically reviewed.
- Not be shared.

---

# 7. Privileged Access Management

Administrative accounts shall:

- Be limited to authorized administrators.
- Use dedicated privileged accounts.
- Require Multi-Factor Authentication.
- Be monitored continuously.
- Be reviewed quarterly.
- Be removed when no longer required.

---

# 8. Authentication Requirements

Authentication shall include:

- Strong passwords
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Secure password storage
- Account lockout
- Session timeout
- Secure authentication protocols

---

# 9. Authorization Requirements

Access approval shall include:

- Business justification
- Manager approval
- System owner approval
- Appropriate role assignment
- Periodic validation

---

# 10. Remote Access

Remote access shall:

- Use VPN or secure remote solutions.
- Require MFA.
- Be encrypted.
- Be monitored.
- Be restricted to approved devices.

---

# 11. Third-Party Access

Third-party access shall:

- Require contracts.
- Require confidentiality agreements.
- Be time-limited.
- Be monitored.
- Be reviewed regularly.
- Be revoked upon contract completion.

---

# 12. Monitoring and Logging

The organization shall monitor:

- Login attempts
- Failed logins
- Privileged activities
- Account creation
- Account deletion
- Permission changes
- Authentication failures

Logs shall be retained according to enterprise logging standards.

---

# 13. Compliance

This policy supports compliance with:

- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022
- CIS Controls v8
- COBIT 2019
- Applicable legal and regulatory requirements

---

# 14. Exceptions

Exceptions shall:

- Be documented.
- Include business justification.
- Be approved by Executive Management.
- Include compensating controls.
- Be reviewed periodically.

---

# 15. Roles and Responsibilities

### Executive Management

- Approve access control strategy.

### Chief Information Security Officer

- Own this policy.
- Oversee implementation.

### System Owners

- Approve access requests.
- Review user access.

### Managers

- Approve employee access.
- Notify HR and IT of role changes.

### Users

- Protect credentials.
- Report unauthorized access.
- Follow security policies.

---

# 16. Review

This policy shall be reviewed:

- Annually
- Following significant security incidents
- Following regulatory changes
- Following major technology changes

# 19. Definitions

**Least Privilege** — Users receive only the access necessary to perform assigned duties.

**Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)** — Access is granted according to job responsibilities.

**Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)** — Authentication using two or more verification methods.

**Privileged Account** — An account with elevated administrative permissions.
