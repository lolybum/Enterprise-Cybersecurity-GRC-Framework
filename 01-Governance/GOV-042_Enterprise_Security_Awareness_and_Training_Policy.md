# GOV-042 Enterprise Security Awareness and Training Policy

**Document ID:** GOV-042

**Version:** 1.0

**Owner:** Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)

**Approved By:** Executive Management

**Effective Date:** July 2026

**Review Cycle:** Annual

**Classification:** Internal

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Enterprise Security Awareness and Training Policy is to establish governance requirements for developing, delivering, managing, and continuously improving cybersecurity awareness and training programs that strengthen the organization's security culture and reduce human-related cybersecurity risk.

This policy establishes a standardized enterprise security awareness framework that promotes secure behavior, improves employee understanding of cybersecurity responsibilities, supports regulatory compliance, and helps protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of organizational information and technology assets.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All employees.
- Contractors.
- Consultants.
- Temporary workers.
- Interns.
- Third-party vendors.
- Managed service providers.
- Business partners with access to organizational information.
- Executive leadership.
- Information Technology personnel.
- Information Security personnel.
- Security Operations Center (SOC) personnel.
- Developers.
- Cloud administrators.
- Identity and Access Management (IAM) administrators.
- Privileged users.
- Remote workers.

This policy applies to all personnel who access, process, store, transmit, or manage organizational information or technology resources.

---

# 3. Objectives

The objectives of this policy are to:

- Establish enterprise security awareness governance.
- Promote a strong cybersecurity culture.
- Improve employee awareness of cyber threats.
- Reduce human-related cybersecurity risks.
- Support secure use of organizational information systems.
- Strengthen phishing and social engineering awareness.
- Support compliance with legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements.
- Improve incident reporting by employees.
- Ensure role-based security training is provided to personnel with specialized responsibilities.
- Continuously improve organizational cybersecurity awareness.

---

# 4. Policy Statement

The organization shall maintain a formal Enterprise Security Awareness and Training Program that provides ongoing cybersecurity education, awareness, and role-based training to personnel.

Security awareness activities shall be risk-based, documented, regularly reviewed, and integrated with Human Resources, Information Security, Risk Management, Compliance, and Enterprise Governance functions.

All personnel shall complete required security awareness training upon hire and annually thereafter. Personnel with elevated cybersecurity responsibilities shall complete additional role-based training appropriate to their job functions.

The organization shall regularly evaluate the effectiveness of the Security Awareness and Training Program and update content to address emerging cyber threats, technology changes, regulatory requirements, and lessons learned from security incidents.

---

# 5. Security Awareness Governance

The organization shall maintain a formal Security Awareness and Training Program to oversee the planning, development, delivery, measurement, and continuous improvement of cybersecurity awareness activities.

Security awareness governance shall include:

- Executive sponsorship.
- Defined governance roles and responsibilities.
- Enterprise security awareness standards.
- Risk-based training requirements.
- Regulatory compliance oversight.
- Program performance monitoring.
- Periodic management reviews.
- Enterprise-wide communication.
- Metrics and reporting.
- Continuous improvement initiatives.

The Security Awareness Program shall align with Enterprise Risk Management, Information Security, Human Resources, and Compliance functions.

---

# 6. Security Awareness Program

The organization shall maintain an enterprise-wide cybersecurity awareness program that educates personnel on cybersecurity risks and secure behaviors.

The awareness program shall include:

- Security awareness campaigns.
- Annual security awareness training.
- Monthly security reminders.
- Security newsletters.
- Cybersecurity awareness events.
- Posters and digital communications.
- Executive awareness sessions.
- Emerging threat awareness.
- Regulatory awareness.
- Continuous education initiatives.

Awareness content shall be reviewed and updated regularly to address evolving threats.

---

# 7. Security Training

Role-based cybersecurity training shall be provided based on job responsibilities and access to organizational assets.

Training requirements include:

- New hire security orientation.
- Annual refresher training.
- Role-based security training.
- Privileged user training.
- Secure software development training.
- Cloud security training.
- Incident response training.
- Data protection training.
- Privacy awareness training.
- Third-party security training where applicable.

Completion of required security training shall be documented and monitored.

---

# 8. Phishing Awareness

The organization shall educate personnel on phishing, business email compromise, social engineering, and related cyber threats.

Phishing awareness activities include:

- Simulated phishing campaigns.
- Email security awareness.
- Social engineering awareness.
- SMS phishing (Smishing) awareness.
- Voice phishing (Vishing) awareness.
- QR code phishing awareness.
- Reporting suspicious messages.
- Credential protection.
- Safe web browsing.
- Lessons learned following phishing exercises.

Results from phishing simulations shall be used to improve awareness and reduce organizational risk.

---

# 9. Role-Based Training

Specialized security training shall be provided for personnel with elevated cybersecurity responsibilities.

Role-based training shall include:

- Executive leadership.
- System administrators.
- Security Operations Center (SOC) analysts.
- Incident Response Team members.
- Developers.
- Cloud administrators.
- Database administrators.
- Identity and Access Management (IAM) administrators.
- Third-party management personnel.
- Information Security personnel.

Training shall be reviewed periodically to ensure alignment with evolving technologies and threats.

---

# 10. Training Records

The organization shall maintain records demonstrating completion of required security awareness and training activities.

Training records shall include:

- Employee name.
- Training completed.
- Completion date.
- Training status.
- Assessment results.
- Refresher training history.
- Certification where applicable.
- Manager acknowledgement.
- Exceptions and waivers.
- Audit evidence.

Training records shall be retained in accordance with the Enterprise Data Retention and Secure Disposal Policy.

---

# 11. Security Awareness Communications

The organization shall communicate cybersecurity information regularly to reinforce secure behaviors and promote awareness of emerging threats.

Security awareness communications shall include:

- Security newsletters.
- Email security alerts.
- Emerging threat advisories.
- Security bulletins.
- Awareness campaigns.
- Cybersecurity Awareness Month activities.
- Executive security messages.
- Security posters and digital signage.
- Internal collaboration platform announcements.
- Lessons learned from security incidents.

Communications shall be timely, relevant, and appropriate for the intended audience.

---

# 12. Training Effectiveness

The effectiveness of the Security Awareness and Training Program shall be measured using defined performance metrics.

Program effectiveness measures include:

- Training completion rates.
- Assessment scores.
- Phishing simulation results.
- Security incident reporting rates.
- Policy compliance metrics.
- Audit findings.
- User feedback.
- Reduction in repeat security awareness failures.
- Role-based training completion.
- Regulatory compliance metrics.

Program metrics shall be reviewed periodically to identify opportunities for improvement.

---

# 13. Third-Party Awareness Requirements

Third parties with access to organizational systems or information shall complete security awareness training appropriate to the services they provide.

Third-party awareness requirements include:

- Security awareness during onboarding.
- Confidentiality obligations.
- Acceptable use requirements.
- Incident reporting procedures.
- Data protection responsibilities.
- Regulatory awareness.
- Secure remote access practices.
- Social engineering awareness.
- Annual refresher training where applicable.
- Documentation of training completion.

Third-party awareness requirements shall align with the Enterprise Third-Party Security Policy.

---

# 14. Metrics and Reporting

The organization shall maintain metrics to evaluate the effectiveness of the Enterprise Security Awareness and Training Program.

Program metrics may include:

- Training participation rates.
- Completion percentages.
- Phishing simulation success rates.
- Repeat phishing susceptibility.
- Incident reporting trends.
- Security awareness campaign participation.
- Audit findings.
- Compliance reporting.
- Executive dashboards.
- Continuous improvement metrics.

Metrics shall be reported periodically to Executive Management and the Governance Committee.

---

# 15. Compliance Monitoring

Compliance with security awareness requirements shall be monitored continuously.

Monitoring activities include:

- Training completion tracking.
- Policy acknowledgment reviews.
- Phishing campaign monitoring.
- Role-based training validation.
- Audit assessments.
- Regulatory compliance reviews.
- Manager compliance reporting.
- Exception monitoring.
- Corrective action tracking.
- Executive reporting.

Non-compliance shall be addressed in accordance with enterprise disciplinary procedures.

# 16. Roles and Responsibilities

## Executive Management

Executive Management shall:

- Approve the Enterprise Security Awareness Program.
- Allocate appropriate resources.
- Promote a strong security culture.
- Review program performance.

## Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)

The CISO shall:

- Own this policy.
- Oversee enterprise security awareness governance.
- Approve awareness strategies.
- Review program metrics.
- Report cybersecurity awareness risks to Executive Management.

## Information Security Team

The Information Security Team shall:

- Develop awareness content.
- Deliver awareness campaigns.
- Conduct phishing simulations.
- Monitor training effectiveness.
- Recommend program improvements.
- Maintain awareness documentation.

## Human Resources

Human Resources shall:

- Coordinate new hire security training.
- Track mandatory training completion.
- Maintain training records.
- Support policy acknowledgments.
- Coordinate corrective actions for non-compliance.

## Managers

Managers shall:

- Ensure personnel complete required training.
- Support awareness initiatives.
- Monitor team compliance.
- Reinforce secure behaviors.
- Escalate repeated non-compliance.

## Employees

Employees shall:

- Complete required security awareness training.
- Participate in phishing simulations.
- Report suspected security incidents.
- Follow enterprise security policies.
- Protect organizational information and assets.

# 18. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy shall:

- Be formally documented.
- Include business justification.
- Include a documented risk assessment.
- Identify compensating security controls.
- Be approved by the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO).
- Include an expiration date.
- Be reviewed annually.

Approved exceptions shall be maintained within the Enterprise Exception Register.

# 22. Definitions

**Security Awareness** – Activities designed to increase personnel understanding of cybersecurity risks, policies, procedures, and secure behaviors.

**Security Training** – Structured education that provides personnel with the knowledge and skills required to securely perform their job responsibilities.

**Phishing Simulation** – An authorized exercise designed to evaluate and improve employee ability to recognize and report phishing attempts.

**Role-Based Training** – Specialized security training tailored to the responsibilities and access privileges of specific job roles.

**Security Culture** – The collective attitudes, behaviors, and practices that influence how personnel protect organizational information and technology assets.

**Social Engineering** – Techniques used to manipulate individuals into disclosing confidential information or performing actions that compromise security.
