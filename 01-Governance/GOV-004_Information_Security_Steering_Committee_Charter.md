# GOV-004 Information Security Steering Committee Charter

**Document ID:** GOV-004  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Classification:** Internal Use  
**Owner:** Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)  
**Approved By:** Executive Leadership Team  
**Effective Date:** July 2026  
**Review Cycle:** Annual

---

# 1. Purpose

The Information Security Steering Committee (ISSC) provides executive oversight for Apex Technologies' Information Security Governance Program. The committee ensures that cybersecurity initiatives align with business objectives, regulatory obligations, and organizational risk tolerance.

The ISSC serves as the primary governance body responsible for strategic cybersecurity direction, enterprise security priorities, and oversight of information security performance.

---

# 2. Mission

The mission of the Information Security Steering Committee is to:

- Provide executive leadership for cybersecurity governance.
- Align security initiatives with business objectives.
- Oversee enterprise cyber risk.
- Review information security policies and standards.
- Ensure compliance with applicable regulatory requirements.
- Support secure digital transformation initiatives.

---

# 3. Scope

This charter applies to:

- Executive Leadership
- Information Security
- Information Technology
- Risk Management
- Compliance
- Legal
- Human Resources
- Finance
- Business Unit Leaders
- Third-party service providers where applicable.

---

# 4. Objectives

The committee shall:

- Establish cybersecurity priorities.
- Review enterprise cyber risks.
- Monitor regulatory compliance.
- Approve governance documents.
- Review security metrics.
- Support incident response governance.
- Allocate strategic security resources.
- Promote cybersecurity awareness across the organization.

---

# 5. Authority

The Steering Committee is authorized by Executive Management to:

- Approve enterprise security strategies.
- Recommend policy changes.
- Escalate unacceptable risks.
- Review audit findings.
- Track remediation activities.
- Require corrective action from business units.

---

# 6. Committee Membership

The Information Security Steering Committee shall consist of the following members:

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Chief Executive Officer (CEO) | Executive Sponsor |
| Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) | Committee Chair |
| Chief Information Officer (CIO) | IT Strategy Alignment |
| Chief Risk Officer (CRO) | Enterprise Risk Oversight |
| General Counsel | Legal & Regulatory Guidance |
| Director of Compliance | Regulatory Compliance |
| Human Resources Director | Personnel Security |
| Finance Director | Budget and Investment Oversight |
| Business Unit Representatives | Business Alignment |

Additional subject matter experts may attend meetings as required.

---

# 7. Roles and Responsibilities

## Committee Chair

The Committee Chair shall:

- Schedule committee meetings.
- Approve meeting agendas.
- Review enterprise cyber risks.
- Present strategic recommendations.
- Report to Executive Leadership.

## Committee Members

Members shall:

- Attend scheduled meetings.
- Review security reports.
- Participate in decision-making.
- Support security initiatives.
- Monitor remediation efforts.
- Promote cybersecurity awareness.

## Information Security Team

The Information Security Team shall:

- Prepare committee reports.
- Track security metrics.
- Maintain the enterprise risk register.
- Coordinate policy updates.
- Monitor compliance activities.

---

# 8. Meeting Schedule

The committee shall meet:

- Monthly
- Following major security incidents
- Prior to significant technology initiatives
- Following external audit findings
- At the request of Executive Leadership

Meeting minutes shall be maintained for all committee meetings.

---

# 9. Decision-Making

Decisions shall be made by majority vote of committee members.

The Chair shall have final authority when consensus cannot be reached.

All significant decisions shall be documented within meeting minutes.

---

# 10. Reporting

The committee shall provide regular reports covering:

- Enterprise cyber risk
- Security incidents
- Compliance status
- Audit findings
- Risk treatment progress
- Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Key Risk Indicators (KRIs)

---

# 11. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The committee shall monitor the following security performance indicators:

- Security awareness training completion rate
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) adoption
- Critical vulnerability remediation within SLA
- Mean Time to Detect (MTTD)
- Mean Time to Respond (MTTR)
- Security incident closure rate
- Third-party security assessment completion
- Policy review completion rate
- Audit finding remediation status

---

# 12. Risk Oversight

The committee shall oversee:

- Enterprise cyber risk register
- Strategic technology risks
- Third-party/vendor risks
- Cloud security risks
- Regulatory compliance risks
- Business continuity risks
- Disaster recovery readiness
- Emerging cybersecurity threats

Risk acceptance decisions shall align with the organization's approved risk appetite.

---

# 13. Policy Governance

The committee shall review and approve:

- Information Security Policies
- Standards
- Procedures
- Guidelines
- Security Baselines
- Security Exceptions
- Governance Framework updates

Policies shall be reviewed at least annually or whenever significant business or regulatory changes occur.

---

# 14. Incident Oversight

Following significant cybersecurity incidents, the committee shall:

- Review root cause analysis
- Evaluate business impact
- Monitor corrective actions
- Review lessons learned
- Track remediation activities
- Approve strategic improvements

---

# 15. Compliance Oversight

The committee shall monitor compliance with:

- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022
- CIS Controls v8
- COBIT 2019
- Applicable legal and regulatory requirements

---

# 16. Vendor Security Oversight

The committee shall oversee:

- Vendor risk assessments
- Third-party due diligence
- Security contract requirements
- Critical supplier monitoring
- Vendor remediation plans
- Third-party security incidents

---

# 17. Security Metrics Review

The committee shall review the following metrics at each meeting:

- Number of security incidents
- Mean Time to Detect (MTTD)
- Mean Time to Respond (MTTR)
- Vulnerability remediation performance
- Patch compliance
- Security awareness training completion
- Third-party risk assessment status
- Compliance audit results
- High-risk findings outstanding

---

# 18. Continuous Improvement

The committee shall support continual improvement by:

- Reviewing governance maturity
- Evaluating security program effectiveness
- Monitoring technology trends
- Reviewing threat intelligence
- Supporting innovation in cybersecurity
- Tracking strategic initiatives

---

# 19. Exceptions

Exceptions to this charter shall:

- Be documented.
- Include business justification.
- Be approved by the CISO.
- Be reviewed by the Steering Committee.
- Include an expiration date.
- Be recorded within the enterprise exception register.

---

# 20. Enforcement

Failure to comply with assigned security responsibilities may result in:

- Security retraining
- Access removal
- Disciplinary action
- Contract termination
- Legal action where applicable
