# GOV-013 Enterprise Security Awareness and Training Policy

**Document ID:** GOV-013  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)  
**Approved By:** Executive Management  
**Effective Date:** July 2026  
**Review Cycle:** Annual

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish enterprise requirements for cybersecurity awareness, education, and role-based security training. A comprehensive awareness program helps reduce human-related cybersecurity risks, strengthens the organization's security culture, and supports compliance with regulatory and industry requirements.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All employees
- Contractors
- Third-party service providers
- Temporary personnel
- Interns
- Consultants
- Business partners with system access
- Executive Management
- Members of the Board of Directors
- All organizational information systems

---

# 3. Objectives

The organization shall:

- Promote a strong cybersecurity culture.
- Increase employee security awareness.
- Reduce phishing and social engineering risks.
- Improve incident reporting.
- Strengthen regulatory compliance.
- Support secure handling of organizational information.
- Improve overall cybersecurity resilience.

---

# 4. Policy Statement

The organization shall maintain a formal enterprise Security Awareness and Training Program designed to educate personnel on cybersecurity threats, organizational policies, secure behaviors, and regulatory obligations.

Security awareness training shall be mandatory for all workforce members.

---

# 5. Security Awareness Program

The Security Awareness Program shall include:

- New employee security orientation
- Annual security awareness training
- Role-based cybersecurity training
- Executive security awareness sessions
- Phishing awareness education
- Social engineering awareness
- Secure password practices
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) awareness
- Data protection awareness
- Insider threat awareness

---

# 6. Training Requirements

Security awareness training shall:

- Be completed during employee onboarding.
- Be completed annually by all personnel.
- Be updated to address emerging cyber threats.
- Include regulatory and compliance requirements.
- Be documented within the enterprise learning management system.
- Be tracked for completion.
- Be mandatory for continued system access where applicable.

---

# 7. Phishing Simulation Program

The organization shall conduct periodic phishing simulations to:

- Measure employee awareness.
- Identify users requiring additional training.
- Improve resistance to phishing attacks.
- Reinforce secure email practices.
- Reduce credential theft risks.
- Measure organizational security maturity.

Simulation results shall be reviewed by Information Security and reported to Executive Management.

---

# 8. Specialized Role-Based Training

Additional cybersecurity training shall be provided for personnel with elevated responsibilities, including:

- Executive Management
- Information Security personnel
- System Administrators
- Network Administrators
- Database Administrators
- Cloud Administrators
- Software Developers
- Help Desk personnel
- Human Resources
- Finance personnel
- Third-party administrators

Training shall be tailored to each role's security responsibilities.

---

# 9. Incident Reporting Awareness

Personnel shall be trained to recognize and report:

- Phishing emails
- Malware infections
- Social engineering attempts
- Unauthorized access
- Lost or stolen devices
- Data breaches
- Suspicious system activity
- Insider threat indicators

Suspected cybersecurity incidents shall be reported immediately using the organization's approved incident reporting process.

---

# 10. Compliance Requirements

The Security Awareness Program shall support compliance with:

- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022
- CIS Controls v8
- COBIT 2019
- Applicable legal and regulatory requirements

Training records shall be retained in accordance with enterprise record retention requirements.

---

# 11. Program Effectiveness

The Security Awareness and Training Program shall be evaluated through:

- Training completion rates
- Phishing simulation results
- Security awareness surveys
- Incident reporting trends
- Audit findings
- Compliance assessments
- Lessons learned from cybersecurity incidents

Program improvements shall be implemented based on assessment results.

---

# 12. Roles and Responsibilities

## Executive Management

- Support the enterprise Security Awareness Program.
- Ensure adequate funding and resources.
- Review security awareness metrics.

## Information Security

- Develop and maintain training content.
- Conduct phishing simulations.
- Monitor program effectiveness.
- Maintain training records.
- Report program performance to management.

## Managers

- Ensure personnel complete required training.
- Reinforce secure behaviors within their teams.
- Support remediation activities when training deficiencies are identified.

## Employees and Contractors

- Complete all required security awareness training.
- Follow enterprise security policies.
- Report suspected cybersecurity incidents promptly.
- Apply secure practices when handling organizational information.

---

# 13. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy shall:

- Be documented.
- Include business justification.
- Be approved by Executive Management.
- Include compensating security controls where appropriate.
- Include an expiration date.
- Be reviewed periodically.

Approved exceptions shall be maintained within the enterprise exception register.

---

# 14. Continuous Improvement

The Security Awareness Program shall be continuously improved through:

- Internal audits
- External audits
- Threat intelligence
- Regulatory updates
- Employee feedback
- Lessons learned from incidents
- Emerging cybersecurity risks
- Annual management reviews

---

# 15. Security Metrics and Reporting

The Information Security team shall report program metrics including:

- Training completion rates
- Phishing simulation success rates
- Repeat phishing failures
- Incident reporting statistics
- Compliance status
- Outstanding training requirements
- Program maturity improvements

Metrics shall be reported periodically to Executive Management and the Information Security Steering Committee.

---

# 16. Audit and Compliance Monitoring

Compliance with this policy shall be verified through:

- Internal audits
- External audits
- Compliance reviews
- Training record inspections
- Phishing simulation assessments
- Management reviews
- Corrective action tracking

Audit findings shall be documented and remediated according to the enterprise audit management process.

# 19. Definitions

**Security Awareness** – Activities designed to increase employees' understanding of cybersecurity threats and safe computing practices.

**Security Training** – Formal instruction that develops the knowledge and skills required to perform job responsibilities securely.

**Phishing** – A social engineering attack that attempts to obtain sensitive information through deceptive communications.

**Social Engineering** – Psychological manipulation used to persuade individuals to disclose confidential information or perform actions that compromise security.

**Role-Based Training** – Security training tailored to specific job functions and responsibilities.
