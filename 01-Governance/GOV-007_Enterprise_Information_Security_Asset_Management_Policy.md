# GOV-007 Enterprise Information Security Asset Management Policy

**Document ID:** GOV-007
**Version:** 1.0
**Owner:** Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)
**Approved By:** Executive Management
**Effective Date:** July 2026
**Review Cycle:** Annual

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish enterprise requirements for identifying, classifying, protecting, maintaining, and disposing of information assets throughout their lifecycle. Proper asset management ensures that business information remains confidential, accurate, available, and protected from unauthorized access, modification, disclosure, or destruction.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All employees
- Contractors
- Consultants
- Temporary staff
- Third-party service providers
- Business units
- Information assets
- Hardware assets
- Software assets
- Cloud assets
- Mobile devices
- Removable media
- Data repositories

---

# 3. Objectives

The organization shall:

- Maintain an accurate inventory of information assets.
- Assign ownership to all critical assets.
- Classify assets according to business value.
- Protect assets using appropriate security controls.
- Monitor asset lifecycle activities.
- Support regulatory compliance.
- Reduce cybersecurity risk.
- Improve operational resilience.

---

# 4. Policy Statement

Enterprise information assets are valuable business resources and shall be managed throughout their lifecycle in accordance with approved governance requirements.

Every asset shall have:

- An assigned owner
- A documented business purpose
- Appropriate classification
- Required security controls
- Defined lifecycle management
- Periodic review
- Secure disposal process

---

# 5. Asset Classification

Information assets shall be classified as:

- Public
- Internal
- Confidential
- Restricted

Classification shall determine:

- Storage requirements
- Access permissions
- Encryption requirements
- Transmission controls
- Retention requirements
- Disposal procedures

---

# 6. Asset Ownership

Every information asset shall have an assigned owner responsible for:

- Asset classification
- Access authorization
- Periodic review
- Security requirements
- Risk acceptance where applicable
- Regulatory compliance
- Asset lifecycle decisions

Asset owners shall review ownership annually or whenever business responsibilities change.

---

# 7. Asset Inventory Management

The organization shall maintain a centralized inventory of all enterprise information assets.

The inventory shall include:

- Asset ID
- Asset Name
- Asset Type
- Business Owner
- Custodian
- Classification
- Physical or Logical Location
- Business Function
- Criticality Rating
- Lifecycle Status

The inventory shall be reviewed at least annually.

---

# 8. Asset Lifecycle Management

Information assets shall be managed throughout the following lifecycle stages:

- Acquisition
- Registration
- Classification
- Deployment
- Maintenance
- Monitoring
- Retirement
- Secure Disposal

Asset lifecycle activities shall be documented.

---

# 9. Asset Protection

Enterprise information assets shall be protected using appropriate security controls including:

- Access controls
- Encryption
- Multi-factor authentication
- Endpoint protection
- Vulnerability management
- Security monitoring
- Backup and recovery
- Physical security controls

Protection requirements shall be based on asset classification.

---

# 10. Access Control

Access to information assets shall:

- Follow least privilege principles
- Require business justification
- Be approved by the asset owner
- Be reviewed periodically
- Be removed immediately upon termination or role change
- Be monitored for unauthorized activity

Privileged access shall require additional approval and monitoring.

---

# 11. Asset Monitoring

The organization shall continuously monitor information assets for:

- Unauthorized access
- Configuration changes
- Security events
- Vulnerability exposure
- Software integrity
- Hardware health
- Compliance status

Monitoring activities shall support incident detection and risk management.

---

# 12. Asset Maintenance

Information assets shall be maintained through:

- Security patching
- Firmware updates
- Software upgrades
- Configuration reviews
- Vulnerability remediation
- Capacity planning
- Performance optimization

Maintenance activities shall follow approved change management procedures.

---

# 13. Asset Disposal

Information assets reaching end-of-life shall be securely disposed of.

Disposal activities shall include:

- Secure data destruction
- Removal from inventory
- Recovery of licenses
- Hardware sanitization
- Certificate of destruction where applicable
- Documentation of disposal activities

No asset containing organizational information shall be discarded without approved sanitization.

---

# 14. Compliance

This policy supports compliance with:

- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022
- CIS Controls v8
- COBIT 2019
- Applicable legal and regulatory requirements

Compliance shall be monitored through periodic audits and management reviews.

---

# 15. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy shall:

- Be documented
- Include business justification
- Be approved by Executive Management
- Include compensating controls
- Include an expiration date
- Be reviewed periodically

All approved exceptions shall be maintained within the enterprise exception register.

---

# 16. Continuous Improvement

The enterprise asset management program shall be continuously improved through:

- Lessons learned
- Internal audits
- External audits
- Risk assessments
- Security incident reviews
- Vulnerability management
- Technology improvements
- Annual policy reviews
- Management feedback

# 19. Definitions

**Information Asset** – Any information, system, application, hardware, software, or service that has value to the organization.

**Asset Owner** – Individual responsible for the business value, classification, protection, and lifecycle of an information asset.

**Asset Custodian** – Individual or team responsible for maintaining and safeguarding an information asset.

**Asset Classification** – The process of assigning sensitivity and business value to an information asset.
