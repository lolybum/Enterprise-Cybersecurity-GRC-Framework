

# GOV-014 Enterprise Information Security Incident Management Policy

**Document ID:** GOV-014  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)  
**Approved By:** Executive Management  
**Effective Date:** July 2026  
**Review Cycle:** Annual

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Enterprise Information Security Incident Management Policy is to establish a structured and consistent approach for identifying, reporting, assessing, responding to, recovering from, and documenting information security incidents. The policy ensures that security incidents are managed effectively to minimize operational disruption, protect organizational information assets, preserve evidence, satisfy legal and regulatory obligations, and improve the organization's overall cybersecurity resilience.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All employees
- Contractors
- Consultants
- Temporary workers
- Third-party service providers
- Cloud service providers
- Business partners with access to organizational systems
- All information systems
- Applications
- Networks
- Cloud environments
- End-user devices
- Information assets owned, processed, or managed by Apex Technologies

## 3. Objectives

The objectives of this policy are to:

- Detect security incidents as early as possible.
- Minimize operational disruption.
- Protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information.
- Ensure timely incident reporting.
- Preserve digital evidence.
- Meet regulatory and contractual obligations.
- Support business continuity and disaster recovery.
- Improve incident response capabilities through continuous improvement.

## 4. Policy Statement

Apex Technologies shall maintain a formal Information Security Incident Management Program to ensure all suspected or confirmed information security incidents are managed consistently throughout their lifecycle.

All personnel are required to immediately report suspected or confirmed security incidents through approved reporting channels.

Every incident shall be:

- Reported
- Logged
- Assessed
- Classified
- Contained
- Investigated
- Eradicated
- Recovered
- Documented
- Reviewed after resolution

Incident response activities shall follow documented procedures approved by Executive Management and coordinated by the Information Security function.

## 5. Incident Classification

Information security incidents shall be classified according to their business impact and urgency.

### Critical
- Enterprise-wide outage
- Ransomware attack
- Major data breach
- Active compromise of privileged accounts

### High
- Malware infection
- Unauthorized access to sensitive systems
- Service disruption affecting multiple departments

### Medium
- Phishing email with limited impact
- Unauthorized software installation
- Policy violations

### Low
- Minor security events
- Spam emails
- Failed login attempts without compromise

Classification determines:

- Response priority
- Escalation path
- Required notifications
- Investigation timeline

## 6. Incident Reporting

All employees shall immediately report suspected or confirmed security incidents through approved reporting channels.

Reports should include:

- Date and time
- System affected
- Description of the incident
- Reporter information
- Screenshots or evidence where available

Failure to report security incidents may result in disciplinary action.

## 7. Incident Response Process

The organization's Incident Response Process shall follow a structured lifecycle to ensure effective handling of information security incidents.

The phases include:

- Preparation
- Detection and Analysis
- Containment
- Eradication
- Recovery
- Lessons Learned

Each incident shall:

- Be assigned an incident owner
- Be documented throughout its lifecycle
- Follow approved response procedures
- Include appropriate escalation where required
- Be closed only after validation that systems have been restored

Incident response activities shall be tested periodically through tabletop exercises and technical simulations.

## 8. Roles and Responsibilities

### Executive Management
- Approve the Incident Management Program
- Provide necessary resources
- Review significant incidents

### Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)
- Lead the incident response program
- Coordinate investigations
- Report major incidents to Executive Management

### Information Security Team
- Detect and investigate incidents
- Coordinate containment and recovery
- Preserve evidence
- Maintain incident records

### IT Operations
- Restore affected services
- Support containment activities
- Implement corrective actions

### Employees
- Report suspected incidents immediately
- Preserve potential evidence
- Cooperate during investigations

### Third-Party Providers
- Report incidents affecting organizational data
- Support investigations when required
- Meet contractual incident reporting obligations

## 9. Communication Requirements

All communications regarding information security incidents shall be accurate, timely, and coordinated.

Communication requirements include:

- Immediate notification of the Information Security Team
- Escalation of Critical incidents to Executive Management
- Notification of affected business units
- Communication with customers where required
- Communication with third-party service providers
- Coordination with Legal, Compliance, and Human Resources
- Public communications shall be approved by Executive Management and Corporate Communications

Only authorized personnel may communicate with external parties regarding security incidents.

## 10. Evidence Collection

Digital evidence shall be collected, preserved, and handled in a manner that maintains its integrity.

Evidence collection shall include:

- System logs
- Network logs
- Security alerts
- Memory captures where appropriate
- Disk images when required
- Screenshots
- Audit trails

Chain-of-custody documentation shall be maintained for all evidence that may support legal, regulatory, or disciplinary actions.

## 11. Third-Party Incident Handling

Third-party service providers that process, store, or transmit organizational information shall comply with contractual incident response requirements.

Third parties shall:

- Notify Apex Technologies of security incidents without unreasonable delay
- Preserve evidence related to the incident
- Cooperate during investigations
- Participate in coordinated incident response activities when requested
- Implement corrective actions to prevent recurrence

Service providers shall maintain incident response capabilities consistent with contractual and regulatory requirements.

## 12. Regulatory Notification

Security incidents involving regulated information shall be evaluated to determine applicable legal and regulatory notification requirements.

Notifications may include:

- Regulatory authorities
- Law enforcement agencies
- Customers
- Business partners
- Insurance providers

Notifications shall be coordinated by Legal, Compliance, Executive Management, and the Information Security function.

All required notifications shall be documented.

## 13. Post-Incident Review

Following resolution of every significant security incident, a formal post-incident review shall be conducted.

The review shall include:

- Root cause analysis
- Timeline of events
- Effectiveness of response activities
- Lessons learned
- Control improvements
- Preventive actions
- Policy or procedure updates where necessary

Recommendations shall be tracked until completed.

## 14. Compliance

Compliance with this policy shall be monitored through:

- Internal audits
- Security assessments
- Incident response exercises
- Management reviews
- Regulatory assessments

This policy supports compliance with:

- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022
- CIS Controls v8
- COBIT 2019
- Applicable legal and regulatory requirements

## 15. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy shall:

- Be documented
- Include business justification
- Be approved by Executive Management
- Include compensating controls
- Include an expiration date
- Be reviewed periodically

## 16. Continuous Improvement

The Incident Management Program shall be continuously improved through:

- Incident trend analysis
- Lessons learned
- Threat intelligence
- Security testing
- Tabletop exercises
- Penetration testing results
- Internal audit findings
- Regulatory changes

Management shall periodically review program effectiveness.

## 19. Definitions

**Incident** – An event that compromises or threatens the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of information.

**Security Event** – An observable occurrence relevant to system or network security.

**Evidence** – Information collected during an investigation that supports analysis or legal proceedings.

**Containment** – Actions taken to limit the impact of an incident.

**Recovery** – Activities performed to restore systems to normal operation.
