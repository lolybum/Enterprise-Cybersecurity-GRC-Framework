# GOV-010 Enterprise Password Management Policy

**Document ID:** GOV-010  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)  
**Approved By:** Executive Management  
**Effective Date:** July 2026  
**Review Cycle:** Annual

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish enterprise requirements for the creation, use, protection, storage, and management of passwords. Strong password management reduces the risk of unauthorized access and helps protect organizational information assets.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All employees
- Contractors
- Third-party service providers
- Temporary staff
- Consultants
- All enterprise systems
- Cloud services
- Network devices
- Mobile devices
- Business applications

---

# 3. Objectives

The organization shall:

- Protect user accounts.
- Prevent unauthorized access.
- Enforce strong authentication.
- Reduce password-related attacks.
- Support regulatory compliance.
- Promote secure password practices.

---

# 4. Policy Statement

All users shall create, maintain, and protect passwords in accordance with this policy.

Passwords shall:

- Be unique.
- Be confidential.
- Never be shared.
- Be protected from disclosure.
- Be changed immediately if compromised.

---

# 5. Password Requirements

Passwords shall:

- Be at least 14 characters long.
- Include uppercase letters.
- Include lowercase letters.
- Include numbers.
- Include special characters.
- Avoid dictionary words.
- Avoid personal information.

---

# 6. Password Complexity

Passwords shall not contain:

- User names
- Employee IDs
- Company name
- Sequential numbers
- Common passwords
- Keyboard patterns

Passphrases are encouraged whenever possible.

---

# 7. Password Storage

Passwords shall:

- Never be written on paper.
- Never be stored in browsers without approval.
- Be stored only in approved password managers.
- Be encrypted while stored.
- Never be transmitted in plaintext.

---

# 8. Password Changes

Passwords shall be changed:

- Immediately if compromised.
- Following security incidents.
- After privileged account exposure.
- When required by applicable regulations.

Routine password changes are not required unless risk justifies them.

---

# 9. Multi-Factor Authentication

MFA shall be required for:

- Remote access
- Administrative accounts
- Cloud services
- VPN access
- Financial applications
- Sensitive business systems

---

# 10. Service Accounts

Service account passwords shall:

- Be securely stored.
- Be rotated periodically.
- Be known only to authorized administrators.
- Be monitored.
- Follow enterprise password standards.

---

# 11. Shared Accounts

Shared accounts shall:

- Be prohibited whenever possible.
- Require documented business justification.
- Be approved by management.
- Be monitored.
- Be reviewed periodically.

---

# 12. Password Managers

Only approved enterprise password managers may be used.

Password managers shall:

- Encrypt stored credentials.
- Require MFA.
- Support secure sharing.
- Generate strong passwords.
- Maintain audit logs.

---

# 13. Compliance

This policy supports compliance with:

- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022
- CIS Controls v8
- COBIT 2019
- Applicable legal and regulatory requirements

---

# 14. Exceptions

Exceptions shall:

- Be documented.
- Include business justification.
- Be approved by Executive Management.
- Include compensating controls.
- Be reviewed periodically.

---

# 15. Roles and Responsibilities

### Executive Management

- Approve password management strategy.

### Chief Information Security Officer

- Own this policy.
- Ensure implementation.

### IT Administrators

- Enforce password controls.
- Monitor compliance.

### Managers

- Ensure employees follow this policy.

### Users

- Create strong passwords.
- Protect credentials.
- Report suspected compromise immediately.

---

# 16. Review

This policy shall be reviewed:

- Annually
- Following security incidents
- Following technology changes
- Following regulatory changes

# 19. Definitions

**Password** — A secret authentication credential used to verify identity.

**Passphrase** — A longer sequence of words used as an authentication credential.

**Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)** — Authentication using two or more independent verification methods.

**Password Manager** — An approved application for securely storing and managing passwords.
