# GOV-005 Policy Management Standard

**Document Owner:** Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)

**Approved By:** Executive Leadership

**Version:** 1.0

**Effective Date:** July 2026

**Review Cycle:** Annual

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Policy Management Standard is to establish a consistent, enterprise-wide approach for developing, approving, communicating, maintaining, reviewing, and retiring information security policies.

This standard ensures that all security documentation remains accurate, current, approved by appropriate stakeholders, and aligned with business objectives, regulatory obligations, contractual requirements, and industry best practices.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to:

- All employees
- Contractors
- Temporary staff
- Consultants
- Third-party service providers
- Business partners
- Cloud service providers where applicable

It applies to all enterprise information assets regardless of location or technology platform.

---

# 3. Objectives

This standard establishes requirements to:

- Maintain consistent policy governance.
- Ensure policies support organizational objectives.
- Define ownership and accountability.
- Standardize policy development.
- Ensure periodic review.
- Maintain document version control.
- Support regulatory compliance.
- Promote continuous improvement.

---

# 4. Policy Hierarchy

The enterprise governance documentation shall follow the hierarchy below:

1. Governance Policies
2. Standards
3. Procedures
4. Guidelines
5. Work Instructions
6. Forms and Templates

Each lower-level document shall support higher-level governance requirements.

---

# 5. Policy Ownership

Each policy shall have:

- Document Owner
- Business Owner
- Executive Sponsor
- Review Authority
- Approval Authority

Document owners are responsible for maintaining policy accuracy and ensuring annual review.

---

# 6. Policy Development

All policies shall:

- Use the approved enterprise template.
- Include document metadata.
- Clearly define purpose and scope.
- Identify responsibilities.
- Reference applicable standards.
- Include review frequency.
- Include version history.
