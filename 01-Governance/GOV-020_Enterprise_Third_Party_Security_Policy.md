**Document ID:** GOV-020  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)  
**Approved By:** Executive Management  
**Effective Date:** July 2026  
**Review Cycle:** Annual  
**Classification:** Internal

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Enterprise Third-Party Security Policy is to establish a comprehensive governance framework for managing cybersecurity risks associated with third-party vendors, suppliers, contractors, consultants, cloud service providers, and other external organizations. This policy ensures that third-party relationships are evaluated, monitored, and managed to protect organizational information assets while maintaining compliance with legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All third-party vendors
- Cloud service providers
- Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) providers
- Managed Service Providers (MSPs)
- Managed Security Service Providers (MSSPs)
- Consultants
- Contractors
- Business partners
- Suppliers
- Outsourced service providers
- Technology vendors
- Any external organization that processes, stores, or accesses Apex Technologies information

This policy applies throughout the third-party relationship lifecycle, including onboarding, monitoring, renewal, and termination.

---

# 3. Objectives

The objectives of this policy are to:

- Identify third-party cybersecurity risks.
- Protect organizational information assets.
- Establish standardized vendor risk management practices.
- Ensure regulatory and contractual compliance.
- Reduce supply chain cyber risks.
- Support secure procurement processes.
- Strengthen third-party governance.
- Protect customer and organizational information.
- Improve cyber resilience.
- Promote continuous improvement in third-party security management.

---

# 4. Policy Statement

Apex Technologies shall establish and maintain a comprehensive Third-Party Security Program that identifies, assesses, monitors, and manages cybersecurity risks associated with external organizations.

All third parties shall undergo appropriate security due diligence before access to organizational information or systems is granted.

Third-party relationships shall be continuously monitored based on business criticality, information sensitivity, and risk level.

No third party shall receive access to organizational information without appropriate security controls and contractual protections.

---

# 5. Third-Party Classification

Third parties shall be classified according to the level of organizational risk they present.

Classification factors include:

- Information sensitivity
- System access
- Network connectivity
- Regulatory impact
- Business criticality
- Financial impact
- Operational dependency
- Data processing activities
- Cloud service usage
- Geographic location

Risk categories include:

- Critical Risk
- High Risk
- Moderate Risk
- Low Risk

The classification shall determine the level of security assessment and ongoing monitoring required.

---

# 6. Third-Party Risk Assessment

A cybersecurity risk assessment shall be completed before onboarding any third party.

Assessments shall evaluate:

- Information security program maturity
- Regulatory compliance
- Security certifications
- Privacy controls
- Access management
- Encryption practices
- Incident response capabilities
- Business continuity planning
- Disaster recovery capabilities
- Vulnerability management
- Security awareness training
- Physical security
- Supply chain security

Risk assessments shall be documented and approved before contracts are finalized.

---

# 7. Third-Party Due Diligence

Third-party security due diligence shall be completed before entering into contractual agreements.

Due diligence activities shall include:

- Security questionnaires
- Risk assessments
- Compliance verification
- Financial stability review
- Reference checks
- Security certification review
- Penetration testing reports where appropriate
- Independent audit reports (SOC 2, ISO 27001, etc.)
- Regulatory compliance validation
- Review of prior security incidents

Critical and High-Risk vendors shall undergo enhanced due diligence before approval.

---

# 8. Security Requirements

Third parties shall implement security controls appropriate to the sensitivity of the information they access.

Minimum security requirements include:

- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Strong password policies
- Encryption at rest
- Encryption in transit
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Endpoint protection
- Security monitoring
- Vulnerability management
- Patch management
- Secure system configuration
- Logging and monitoring
- Security awareness training

Third parties shall maintain security controls throughout the duration of the business relationship.

---

# 9. Contractual Security Requirements

Contracts with third parties shall include security clauses addressing:

- Confidentiality
- Information protection
- Data ownership
- Access restrictions
- Incident notification
- Regulatory compliance
- Audit rights
- Right to assess security controls
- Data retention
- Secure data destruction
- Subcontractor management
- Business continuity
- Disaster recovery
- Cyber insurance requirements
- Contract termination obligations

Legal Counsel shall review contractual security requirements before execution.

---

# 10. Access Management

Third-party access shall follow the Principle of Least Privilege.

Access management requirements include:

- Business justification
- Management approval
- Time-limited access where appropriate
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Privileged Access Management (PAM)
- Periodic access reviews
- Immediate revocation upon contract termination

Shared accounts shall not be used.

---

# 11. Continuous Monitoring

Third-party security posture shall be monitored throughout the relationship.

Monitoring activities include:

- Security questionnaires
- Annual risk assessments
- External attack surface monitoring
- Threat intelligence monitoring
- Security rating services
- Compliance reviews
- Vulnerability disclosures
- Security incident tracking
- Regulatory updates
- Contract compliance reviews

Critical vendors shall receive enhanced monitoring based on organizational risk.

---

# 12. Incident Reporting

Third parties shall immediately report security incidents that may affect Apex Technologies.

Incident notifications shall include:

- Nature of the incident
- Systems affected
- Information involved
- Initial impact assessment
- Containment actions
- Recovery activities
- Expected resolution timeline

Incident response activities shall follow the Enterprise Information Security Incident Management Policy.

Third parties shall cooperate fully during investigations.

---

# 13. Compliance Monitoring

Third-party compliance shall be monitored throughout the duration of the business relationship.

Compliance monitoring activities include:

- Periodic security reviews
- Regulatory compliance assessments
- Internal audit reviews
- External audit reports
- Security performance metrics
- Contract compliance validation
- Privacy compliance assessments
- Risk reassessments
- Corrective action tracking

Third parties shall promptly remediate identified compliance deficiencies.

---

# 14. Third-Party Audits

Apex Technologies reserves the right to audit third-party security controls where permitted by contract.

Audit activities may include:

- Security documentation reviews
- Policy reviews
- Technical security assessments
- Vulnerability assessment reports
- Penetration testing reports
- Compliance certifications
- Site visits where appropriate
- Evidence of corrective actions

Audit frequency shall be based on vendor risk classification.

Critical vendors may be audited annually.

---

# 15. Third-Party Offboarding

When a third-party relationship ends, secure offboarding procedures shall be completed.

Offboarding activities include:

- Immediate account deactivation
- Revocation of system access
- Return of organizational assets
- Secure destruction or return of organizational data
- Removal of VPN access
- Revocation of physical access
- Confirmation of contractual obligations
- Documentation of offboarding activities

Data destruction shall be verified and documented.

---

# 16. Roles and Responsibilities

## Executive Management

Executive Management shall:

- Approve the Third-Party Security Program.
- Allocate sufficient resources.
- Review enterprise third-party risks.
- Support supplier risk management initiatives.

## Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)

The CISO shall:

- Own this policy.
- Approve vendor security requirements.
- Review significant third-party risks.
- Report cybersecurity risks to Executive Management.
- Promote continual improvement.

## Vendor Risk Management Team

The Vendor Risk Management Team shall:

- Conduct vendor risk assessments.
- Perform security due diligence.
- Monitor vendor security posture.
- Maintain vendor risk documentation.
- Coordinate vendor reassessments.

## Procurement Team

The Procurement Team shall:

- Ensure security reviews occur before procurement.
- Include required security clauses in contracts.
- Coordinate vendor onboarding.
- Maintain vendor documentation.

## Information Security Team

The Information Security Team shall:

- Evaluate vendor security controls.
- Investigate third-party security incidents.
- Conduct security assessments.
- Support compliance monitoring.
- Recommend remediation actions.

## Business Owners

Business Owners shall:

- Identify vendor business requirements.
- Approve vendor access requests.
- Participate in vendor risk reviews.
- Notify Procurement of vendor changes.

## Third Parties

Third parties shall:

- Comply with contractual security requirements.
- Protect Apex Technologies information.
- Promptly report security incidents.
- Cooperate during audits and investigations.
- Maintain appropriate cybersecurity controls.

---

# 17. Compliance

Compliance with this policy shall be verified through:

- Internal audits
- External audits
- Third-party risk assessments
- Regulatory compliance reviews
- Contract compliance monitoring
- Security questionnaires
- Vendor security assessments
- Executive management reviews

Failure to comply with this policy may result in:

- Corrective action plans
- Suspension of third-party access
- Increased monitoring
- Contractual remedies
- Termination of vendor relationships
- Legal or regulatory action where applicable

---

# 18. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy shall:

- Be formally documented
- Include business justification
- Include a documented risk assessment
- Identify compensating security controls
- Be approved by the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)
- Include an expiration date
- Be reviewed at least annually

Approved exceptions shall be maintained within the Enterprise Exception Register.

---

# 19. Continuous Improvement

The Enterprise Third-Party Security Program shall be continuously improved through:

- Internal audits
- External audits
- Vendor performance reviews
- Threat intelligence
- Supply chain risk assessments
- Security incident lessons learned
- Regulatory updates
- Technology modernization
- Industry best practices

Program effectiveness shall be reviewed annually by Executive Management and the Information Security Steering Committee.

# 22. Definitions

**Third Party** – Any external organization or individual that provides products or services to Apex Technologies or has access to organizational information or systems.

**Vendor Risk Assessment** – A formal evaluation of cybersecurity, operational, privacy, and compliance risks associated with a third party.

**Supply Chain Risk** – The risk that vulnerabilities or compromises within suppliers, vendors, or service providers could adversely affect the organization.

**Due Diligence** – The process of evaluating a third party's security posture, financial stability, compliance, and operational capabilities before engagement.

**Security Questionnaire** – A structured assessment used to evaluate a third party's cybersecurity controls and governance practices.
