# GOV-006 Enterprise Information Security Risk Management Policy

**Document ID:** GOV-006  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)  
**Approved By:** Executive Management  
**Effective Date:** July 2026  
**Review Cycle:** Annual

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish a consistent enterprise approach for identifying, assessing, treating, monitoring, and reporting cybersecurity risks. This policy ensures that information security risks are managed in alignment with business objectives, regulatory obligations, and recognized industry frameworks.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All employees
- Contractors
- Third-party service providers
- Business units
- Information assets
- Cloud services
- On-premises infrastructure
- Software applications
- Data repositories

---

# 3. Objectives

The organization shall:

- Identify cybersecurity risks.
- Evaluate business impact.
- Prioritize risk treatment.
- Reduce enterprise risk exposure.
- Support informed management decisions.
- Meet legal and regulatory requirements.
- Improve organizational resilience.

---

# 4. Risk Management Principles

The organization's cybersecurity risk management program shall be based on the following principles:

- Risk-based decision making.
- Business alignment.
- Continuous improvement.
- Defense in depth.
- Least privilege.
- Regulatory compliance.
- Accountability and ownership.
- Timely risk reporting.

---

# 5. Risk Governance

Executive Management shall:

- Approve the enterprise risk appetite.
- Review significant cybersecurity risks.
- Allocate adequate security resources.
- Support risk treatment initiatives.

The Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) shall:

- Maintain the enterprise cybersecurity risk program.
- Conduct enterprise risk assessments.
- Report significant risks to Executive Management.
- Coordinate risk treatment activities.

Business Unit Managers shall:

- Identify risks within their business functions.
- Support remediation activities.
- Accept or reject risks where authorized.

---

# 6. Risk Assessment Process

The organization shall perform risk assessments that include:

1. Asset identification
2. Threat identification
3. Vulnerability identification
4. Likelihood assessment
5. Business impact assessment
6. Risk calculation
7. Risk prioritization
8. Risk treatment recommendation

Risk assessments shall be conducted:

- At least annually.
- Before major technology implementations.
- Following significant cybersecurity incidents.
- Following major organizational changes.

---

# 7. Risk Categories

Cybersecurity risks shall be categorized into areas including:

- Strategic Risk
- Operational Risk
- Technology Risk
- Third-Party Risk
- Cloud Security Risk
- Regulatory Compliance Risk
- Data Privacy Risk
- Insider Threat Risk
- Business Continuity Risk
- Supply Chain Risk

---

# 8. Risk Treatment

The organization may treat identified risks using one or more of the following approaches:

- Mitigate
- Transfer
- Avoid
- Accept

All accepted risks shall:

- Include documented justification.
- Receive management approval.
- Include an expiration or review date.
- Be recorded in the enterprise risk register.

---

# 9. Risk Register

The organization shall maintain an Enterprise Cybersecurity Risk Register containing:

- Risk ID
- Risk Description
- Asset
- Threat
- Vulnerability
- Likelihood Rating
- Impact Rating
- Overall Risk Score
- Risk Owner
- Treatment Plan
- Target Completion Date
- Current Status

---

# 10. Risk Monitoring

Cybersecurity risks shall be monitored through:

- Continuous security monitoring
- Vulnerability assessments
- Penetration testing
- Security audits
- Compliance reviews
- Threat intelligence
- Security metrics
- Risk dashboards

---

# 11. Risk Reporting

The CISO shall provide regular reports covering:

- Enterprise risk posture
- High-risk findings
- Emerging threats
- Open remediation items
- Risk trends
- Compliance status
- Executive risk metrics

Reports shall be presented quarterly or more frequently when significant risks arise.

---

# 12. Third-Party Risk

Third-party cybersecurity risks shall be managed through:

- Vendor due diligence
- Security questionnaires
- Contractual security requirements
- Risk assessments
- Continuous vendor monitoring
- Annual vendor reviews

---

# 13. Regulatory Compliance

Risk management activities shall support compliance with:

- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022
- CIS Controls v8
- COBIT 2019
- Applicable legal and regulatory requirements

---

# 14. Continuous Improvement

The cybersecurity risk management program shall be continuously improved through:

- Lessons learned
- Incident reviews
- Internal audits
- External audits
- Threat intelligence
- Technology improvements
- Annual policy reviews
- Management feedback

---

# 15. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy shall:

- Be documented.
- Include business justification.
- Be approved by Executive Management.
- Include an expiration date.
- Be reviewed periodically.

---

# 16. Enforcement

Failure to comply with this policy may result in:

- Security retraining
- Access restriction or removal
- Disciplinary action
- Contract termination
- Legal action where applicable

---

# 17. Policy Review

This policy shall be reviewed:

- At least annually.
- Following significant cybersecurity incidents.
- Following major business or regulatory changes.
- Following significant technology changes.

# 20. Definitions

**Risk** – The potential for loss resulting from a threat exploiting a vulnerability.

**Threat** – Any circumstance or event capable of causing harm.

**Vulnerability** – A weakness that may be exploited by a threat.

**Risk Owner** – Individual responsible for managing a specific risk.
