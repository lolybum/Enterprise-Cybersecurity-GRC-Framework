# GOV-048 Enterprise Vendor Risk Management Policy

**Document ID:** GOV-048

**Version:** 1.0

**Owner:** Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)

**Approved By:** Executive Management

**Effective Date:** July 2026

**Review Cycle:** Annual

**Classification:** Internal

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Enterprise Vendor Risk Management Policy is to establish governance requirements for identifying, assessing, monitoring, and managing cybersecurity, operational, privacy, regulatory, and supply chain risks associated with third-party vendors throughout the vendor lifecycle.

This policy establishes a standardized Enterprise Vendor Risk Management (VRM) framework that enables informed risk-based decisions, protects organizational information assets, strengthens supply chain resilience, supports regulatory compliance, and promotes secure vendor relationships.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All employees.
- Contractors.
- Consultants.
- Temporary workers.
- Executive leadership.
- Procurement personnel.
- Information Technology personnel.
- Information Security personnel.
- Legal and Compliance personnel.
- Business owners responsible for vendor relationships.
- Third-party vendors.
- Fourth-party service providers where applicable.

This policy applies to all vendor relationships involving:

- Information technology services.
- Cloud services.
- Software-as-a-Service (SaaS).
- Infrastructure-as-a-Service (IaaS).
- Platform-as-a-Service (PaaS).
- Managed services.
- Professional services.
- Business process outsourcing.
- Data processing services.
- Vendors with access to organizational information or systems.

---

# 3. Objectives

The objectives of this policy are to:

- Establish enterprise vendor risk governance.
- Reduce cybersecurity and supply chain risks.
- Strengthen vendor due diligence.
- Support risk-based vendor management.
- Improve regulatory compliance.
- Protect organizational information.
- Promote secure vendor relationships.
- Monitor vendor security performance.
- Manage vendor risks throughout the lifecycle.
- Support continuous improvement of vendor risk management practices.

---

# 4. Policy Statement

The organization shall maintain a formal Enterprise Vendor Risk Management Program governing the identification, assessment, approval, monitoring, and offboarding of vendors.

Vendor risk management activities shall be risk-based, documented, and integrated with Procurement, Information Security, Legal, Compliance, Privacy, Enterprise Risk Management, and Business functions.

All vendors shall undergo appropriate security and risk assessments before engagement and throughout the duration of the relationship. Vendor access to organizational systems or information shall be granted only after required approvals, contractual safeguards, and security controls have been implemented.

The organization shall continuously monitor vendor risk, review vendor performance, reassess vendor security posture based on risk, and take appropriate corrective actions when vendor risks exceed the organization's established risk tolerance.

---

# 5. Vendor Risk Governance

The organization shall maintain a formal Vendor Risk Management (VRM) governance framework to oversee vendor cybersecurity, operational, privacy, compliance, and supply chain risks.

Vendor risk governance shall include:

- Executive sponsorship.
- Defined governance roles and responsibilities.
- Enterprise vendor risk standards.
- Risk-based vendor oversight.
- Regulatory compliance oversight.
- Vendor performance monitoring.
- Executive reporting.
- Periodic governance reviews.
- Cross-functional collaboration.
- Continuous improvement initiatives.

Vendor risk governance shall align with Enterprise Risk Management, Information Security, Procurement, Legal, Privacy, and Compliance functions.

---

# 6. Vendor Lifecycle Management

Vendor risk shall be managed throughout the entire vendor lifecycle.

Vendor lifecycle phases include:

- Business need identification.
- Vendor selection.
- Due diligence.
- Risk assessment.
- Contract review.
- Vendor onboarding.
- Ongoing monitoring.
- Periodic reassessment.
- Contract renewal.
- Vendor offboarding.

Security requirements shall be incorporated into every stage of the vendor lifecycle.

---

# 7. Vendor Due Diligence

Appropriate due diligence shall be completed before engaging vendors.

Due diligence activities include:

- Vendor security questionnaires.
- Information security assessments.
- Privacy assessments.
- Regulatory compliance reviews.
- Financial stability reviews.
- Business continuity capability reviews.
- Disaster recovery capability reviews.
- Security certification validation.
- Independent audit report reviews (e.g., SOC reports).
- Reference checks.

Due diligence results shall be documented and retained.

---

# 8. Inherent Risk Assessment

All vendors shall undergo an inherent risk assessment before contractual engagement.

Assessment criteria may include:

- Type of services provided.
- Sensitivity of information processed.
- Access to organizational systems.
- Regulatory obligations.
- Geographic location.
- Criticality to business operations.
- Cloud service usage.
- Financial exposure.
- Operational dependency.
- Reputational risk.

Assessment results shall determine the required level of vendor oversight.

---

# 9. Residual Risk Assessment

Residual vendor risk shall be evaluated after security controls and contractual safeguards have been implemented.

Residual risk assessments shall consider:

- Security controls implemented.
- Contractual protections.
- Vendor remediation activities.
- Security testing results.
- Compliance posture.
- Privacy controls.
- Business continuity capabilities.
- Disaster recovery capabilities.
- Monitoring effectiveness.
- Executive risk acceptance where applicable.

Residual risks exceeding organizational risk tolerance shall require documented approval before engagement.

---

# 10. Security Requirements

Vendors shall implement security controls appropriate to the services they provide and the risks they present.

Minimum security requirements include:

- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- Encryption of sensitive information.
- Identity and Access Management (IAM).
- Vulnerability management.
- Patch management.
- Logging and monitoring.
- Incident response capabilities.
- Business continuity planning.
- Disaster recovery capabilities.
- Security awareness training.

Additional security controls may be required based on vendor risk classification.

---

# 11. Contractual Security Requirements

Vendor contracts shall include appropriate cybersecurity, privacy, operational, and compliance requirements.

Contractual security requirements shall include:

- Information security obligations.
- Confidentiality agreements.
- Data protection requirements.
- Privacy compliance requirements.
- Incident notification requirements.
- Right-to-audit provisions.
- Regulatory compliance obligations.
- Security assessment rights.
- Business continuity and disaster recovery requirements.
- Secure data return or destruction upon contract termination.

Contract language shall be reviewed by Legal, Procurement, and Information Security prior to execution.

---

# 12. Fourth-Party Risk Management

The organization shall manage risks associated with subcontractors and other fourth parties that support critical vendors.

Fourth-party risk management shall include:

- Identification of critical subcontractors.
- Contractual flow-down security requirements.
- Fourth-party due diligence where appropriate.
- Security assessments.
- Privacy compliance verification.
- Continuous monitoring.
- Incident notification obligations.
- Business continuity validation.
- Periodic reassessment.
- Documentation of fourth-party relationships.

Critical vendors shall disclose significant subcontractors that may affect organizational risk.

---

# 13. Continuous Vendor Monitoring

Vendor risks shall be monitored throughout the duration of the vendor relationship.

Monitoring activities include:

- Security posture monitoring.
- Vulnerability monitoring.
- Threat intelligence monitoring.
- Compliance monitoring.
- Security rating reviews.
- Regulatory changes.
- Contract compliance reviews.
- Performance monitoring.
- Incident tracking.
- Periodic vendor reassessments.

Monitoring frequency shall be based on vendor criticality and risk level.

---

# 14. Vendor Performance Metrics

The organization shall maintain metrics to evaluate vendor security and operational performance.

Metrics may include:

- Vendor risk ratings.
- Assessment completion rates.
- Remediation completion rates.
- Security incident frequency.
- Regulatory compliance status.
- Service availability.
- SLA performance.
- Audit findings.
- Business continuity testing results.
- Executive reporting.

Vendor performance metrics shall be reviewed periodically to support risk-based decision-making.

---

# 15. Vendor Offboarding

Vendor offboarding shall be completed in a secure and controlled manner.

Offboarding requirements include:

- Revocation of system access.
- Recovery of organizational assets.
- Secure return of organizational information.
- Secure destruction of retained data where required.
- Contract termination review.
- Confirmation of data deletion.
- Closure of vendor accounts.
- Documentation of offboarding activities.
- Final risk review.
- Retention of required vendor records.

Vendor offboarding shall be completed promptly upon termination of the business relationship.

---

# 16. Roles and Responsibilities

## Executive Management

Executive Management shall:

- Approve the Enterprise Vendor Risk Management Program.
- Allocate appropriate resources.
- Review enterprise vendor risks.
- Support continuous improvement initiatives.

## Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)

The CISO shall:

- Own this policy.
- Oversee vendor cybersecurity risk management.
- Approve vendor security standards.
- Review vendor risk metrics.
- Report significant vendor risks to Executive Management.

## Procurement Team

The Procurement Team shall:

- Coordinate vendor onboarding.
- Ensure vendor due diligence is completed.
- Maintain vendor records.
- Coordinate contract reviews.
- Support vendor offboarding.

## Information Security Team

The Information Security Team shall:

- Conduct vendor security assessments.
- Review vendor security controls.
- Monitor vendor cybersecurity risks.
- Recommend remediation activities.
- Support vendor audits.
- Maintain vendor risk documentation.

## Business Owners

Business Owners shall:

- Identify business requirements.
- Sponsor vendor engagements.
- Participate in vendor risk assessments.
- Monitor vendor performance.
- Report vendor-related risks.

## Vendors

Vendors shall:

- Comply with contractual security requirements.
- Protect organizational information.
- Report security incidents promptly.
- Participate in required security assessments.
- Support regulatory and audit activities.

# 17. Compliance

Compliance with this policy shall be verified through:

- Internal audits.
- External audits.
- Vendor risk management assessments.
- Vendor security assessments.
- Regulatory compliance reviews.
- Contract compliance reviews.
- Third-party audit report evaluations.
- Continuous monitoring.
- Vendor performance reviews.
- Executive management reviews.

Failure to comply with this policy may result in:

- Corrective action plans.
- Increased vendor oversight.
- Suspension of vendor access.
- Contract termination.
- Disciplinary action for employees where applicable.
- Legal or regulatory action where applicable.

---

# 18. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy shall:

- Be formally documented.
- Include business justification.
- Include a documented risk assessment.
- Identify compensating security controls.
- Be approved by the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO).
- Be reviewed by Procurement and Legal where applicable.
- Include an expiration date.
- Be reviewed annually.

Approved exceptions shall be maintained within the Enterprise Exception Register.

---

# 19. Continuous Improvement

The Enterprise Vendor Risk Management Program shall be continuously improved through:

- Internal audits.
- External audits.
- Vendor assessment results.
- Security incident lessons learned.
- Regulatory updates.
- Threat intelligence updates.
- Vendor performance reviews.
- Supply chain risk assessments.
- Industry best practices.
- Executive management reviews.

Program effectiveness shall be reviewed annually.

# 22. Definitions

**Vendor** – An external organization or individual that provides products, services, technology, or support to the organization.

**Vendor Risk Management (VRM)** – The process of identifying, assessing, monitoring, and managing risks associated with third-party vendors throughout the vendor lifecycle.

**Inherent Risk** – The level of risk associated with a vendor before security controls or mitigating measures are applied.

**Residual Risk** – The level of risk that remains after security controls, contractual safeguards, and other mitigating measures have been implemented.

**Fourth Party** – A subcontractor or service provider engaged by a third-party vendor to deliver products or services that support the organization's operations.

**Due Diligence** – The process of evaluating a vendor's security, privacy, operational, financial, and regulatory posture before and during the business relationship.
