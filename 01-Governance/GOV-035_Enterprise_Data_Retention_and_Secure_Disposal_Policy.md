# GOV-035 Enterprise Data Retention and Secure Disposal Policy

**Document ID:** GOV-035

**Version:** 1.0

**Owner:** Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)

**Approved By:** Executive Management

**Effective Date:** July 2026

**Review Cycle:** Annual

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Enterprise Data Retention and Secure Disposal Policy is to establish governance requirements for the retention, archival, preservation, and secure disposal of organizational information throughout its lifecycle.

This policy provides a standardized framework to ensure information is retained for appropriate business, legal, contractual, regulatory, and operational purposes while ensuring that information is securely destroyed when it is no longer required.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All employees.
- Contractors.
- Consultants.
- Temporary workers.
- Third-party vendors.
- Managed service providers.
- Business partners.
- Cloud environments.
- On-premises environments.
- Hybrid environments.
- Electronic records.
- Paper records.
- Databases.
- Applications.
- Email systems.
- Backup media.
- Mobile devices.
- Removable media.
- Physical storage locations.

This policy applies to all organizational information regardless of format, ownership, storage location, or transmission method.

---

# 3. Objectives

The objectives of this policy are to:

- Establish enterprise retention requirements.
- Support legal and regulatory compliance.
- Protect organizational records.
- Reduce unnecessary information retention.
- Support litigation readiness.
- Improve records management.
- Ensure secure disposal of information.
- Protect confidential and restricted information.
- Strengthen enterprise information governance.

---

# 4. Policy Statement

The organization shall retain organizational information only for the period required to satisfy business, legal, contractual, operational, and regulatory obligations.

Information that is no longer required shall be securely disposed of using approved destruction methods that prevent unauthorized recovery, disclosure, or reconstruction.

Retention and disposal activities shall be documented, monitored, and periodically reviewed to ensure continued compliance with enterprise governance requirements.

---

# 5. Data Retention Requirements

The organization shall retain information only for approved business, legal, contractual, regulatory, and operational purposes.

Retention requirements include:

- Approved retention schedules.
- Legal and regulatory obligations.
- Business record requirements.
- Contractual obligations.
- Operational requirements.
- Litigation readiness.
- Privacy requirements.
- Industry-specific regulations.
- Executive approval where required.
- Periodic retention reviews.

Information shall not be retained longer than necessary unless specifically required by law or approved business requirements.

---

# 6. Records Management

The organization shall maintain a formal records management program to ensure organizational information is managed consistently throughout its lifecycle.

Records management activities include:

- Record identification.
- Record classification.
- Record indexing.
- Record ownership assignment.
- Version control.
- Metadata management.
- Record retrieval.
- Record protection.
- Record archival.
- Record disposal.

Official records shall be maintained in approved enterprise repositories.

---

# 7. Legal Holds

Information subject to litigation, investigation, audit, or regulatory review shall be preserved through documented legal hold procedures.

Legal hold requirements include:

- Formal legal hold notification.
- Suspension of routine disposal.
- Identification of affected records.
- Preservation of electronic records.
- Preservation of physical records.
- Documentation of legal hold activities.
- Monitoring of legal hold compliance.
- Periodic review.
- Legal department coordination.
- Release of legal hold when authorized.

No information subject to an active legal hold shall be deleted or destroyed.

---

# 8. Data Archiving

Information requiring long-term retention shall be archived using approved enterprise storage solutions.

Archiving requirements include:

- Approved archive repositories.
- Secure storage.
- Encryption where required.
- Access controls.
- Integrity verification.
- Retrieval capabilities.
- Metadata preservation.
- Disaster recovery support.
- Periodic archive validation.
- Documentation of archive activities.

Archived information shall remain accessible throughout the required retention period.

---

# 9. Retention Schedules

The organization shall maintain documented retention schedules for organizational information.

Retention schedules shall define:

- Information category.
- Applicable regulations.
- Business owner.
- Minimum retention period.
- Maximum retention period where applicable.
- Archive requirements.
- Disposal method.
- Approval authority.
- Review frequency.
- Exception handling.

Retention schedules shall be reviewed periodically and updated as legal or business requirements change.

---

# 10. Secure Storage Requirements

Information retained by the organization shall be protected using appropriate administrative, technical, and physical security controls.

Secure storage requirements include:

- Approved storage platforms.
- Encryption of Confidential and Restricted information.
- Access controls.
- Backup protection.
- Physical security.
- Environmental protection.
- Monitoring.
- Integrity validation.
- Disaster recovery capabilities.
- Periodic security assessments.

Stored information shall remain protected against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

---

# 11. Secure Disposal Methods

Information that has reached the end of its approved retention period shall be securely disposed of using approved destruction methods appropriate to its classification and storage medium.

Approved disposal methods include:

- Secure electronic deletion.
- Cryptographic erasure.
- Media sanitization.
- Physical destruction.
- Cross-cut shredding of paper records.
- Pulverization.
- Incineration where permitted.
- Degaussing of magnetic media where applicable.
- Destruction by approved third-party providers.
- Documented disposal verification.

Disposal activities shall prevent unauthorized recovery or reconstruction of information.

---

# 12. Media Sanitization

Storage media shall be sanitized before reuse, reassignment, disposal, or transfer.

Media sanitization requirements include:

- Sanitization according to media type.
- Verification of sanitization.
- Cryptographic erasure where appropriate.
- Secure overwriting.
- Physical destruction for damaged media.
- Chain of custody documentation.
- Inventory updates.
- Secure transportation of media.
- Authorized personnel only.
- Compliance with enterprise media handling standards.

Media sanitization procedures shall align with NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 1.

---

# 13. Certificates of Destruction

Where required, secure disposal activities shall include formal Certificates of Destruction.

Certificates shall include:

- Date of destruction.
- Information category.
- Media type.
- Disposal method.
- Quantity of items destroyed.
- Destruction provider.
- Authorized approver.
- Verification of destruction.
- Chain of custody reference.
- Record retention reference.

Certificates shall be retained according to enterprise records management requirements.

---

# 14. Third-Party Disposal Services

Third-party disposal providers shall be approved through the enterprise vendor management process.

Third-party disposal requirements include:

- Vendor risk assessment.
- Contractual confidentiality obligations.
- Secure transportation procedures.
- Chain of custody documentation.
- Secure destruction verification.
- Certificates of Destruction.
- Regulatory compliance.
- Periodic vendor assessments.
- Audit rights.
- Incident reporting requirements.

Third-party disposal providers shall comply with the Enterprise Third-Party Security Policy.

---

# 15. Monitoring and Auditing

Retention and disposal activities shall be monitored and audited to verify compliance with enterprise governance requirements.

Monitoring activities include:

- Retention schedule compliance.
- Disposal activity reviews.
- Legal hold compliance.
- Archive reviews.
- Media sanitization verification.
- Certificate of Destruction reviews.
- Audit logging.
- Internal audits.
- External audits.
- Regulatory compliance assessments.

Monitoring results shall be reviewed periodically by Information Security and Records Management.

# 16. Roles and Responsibilities

## Executive Management

Executive Management shall:

- Approve the Enterprise Data Retention Program.
- Allocate appropriate resources.
- Review enterprise information governance risks.
- Support continuous improvement initiatives.

## Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)

The CISO shall:

- Own this policy.
- Oversee enterprise retention and disposal governance.
- Approve retention exceptions.
- Review information governance metrics.
- Report significant information governance risks to Executive Management.

## Legal Department

The Legal Department shall:

- Issue legal holds.
- Approve legal hold releases.
- Interpret regulatory retention requirements.
- Support litigation readiness.
- Coordinate with Information Security and Records Management.

## Records Management Team

The Records Management Team shall:

- Maintain retention schedules.
- Coordinate archival activities.
- Manage records repositories.
- Verify disposal activities.
- Maintain records management documentation.

## Information Security Team

The Information Security Team shall:

- Develop secure disposal standards.
- Monitor compliance.
- Verify media sanitization.
- Conduct security assessments.
- Investigate information protection incidents.
- Recommend security improvements.

## Employees

Employees shall:

- Retain information according to approved schedules.
- Protect organizational records.
- Follow secure disposal procedures.
- Comply with legal hold requirements.
- Report suspected information governance violations.

# 18. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy shall:

- Be formally documented.
- Include business justification.
- Include a documented risk assessment.
- Identify compensating security controls.
- Be approved by the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO).
- Include an expiration date.
- Be reviewed annually.

Approved exceptions shall be maintained within the Enterprise Exception Register.

# 22. Definitions

**Data Retention** – The practice of preserving information for a defined period to satisfy business, legal, contractual, and regulatory requirements.

**Records Management** – The governance of organizational records throughout their lifecycle, from creation to secure disposal.

**Legal Hold** – A formal process requiring the preservation of information relevant to litigation, investigations, audits, or regulatory matters.

**Media Sanitization** – The process of permanently removing data from storage media so that it cannot be recovered.

**Certificate of Destruction** – Formal documentation verifying that information or media has been securely destroyed using approved methods.

**Chain of Custody** – A documented record of the possession, transfer, handling, and disposal of information or storage media.
