# GOV-019 Enterprise Data Protection Policy

**Document ID:** GOV-019  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)  
**Approved By:** Executive Management  
**Effective Date:** July 2026  
**Review Cycle:** Annual  
**Classification:** Internal

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Enterprise Data Protection Policy is to establish a comprehensive framework for protecting organizational data throughout its lifecycle. This policy defines the administrative, technical, and physical safeguards necessary to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, destruction, and loss while supporting business continuity, regulatory compliance, and organizational resilience.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All employees
- Contractors
- Consultants
- Third-party service providers
- Cloud service providers
- Business partners handling organizational information
- All information systems
- Servers
- Workstations
- Databases
- Business applications
- Mobile devices
- Cloud environments
- Backup systems
- Collaboration platforms
- Structured and unstructured data

---

# 3. Objectives

The objectives of this policy are to:

- Protect sensitive organizational information.
- Preserve confidentiality, integrity, and availability.
- Reduce the risk of data breaches.
- Ensure compliance with legal and regulatory requirements.
- Protect customer, employee, and business information.
- Establish standardized enterprise data protection controls.
- Secure information throughout its lifecycle.
- Support cyber resilience.
- Enable secure business operations.
- Promote continuous improvement of information protection practices.

---

# 4. Policy Statement

Apex Technologies shall implement enterprise-wide administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect organizational information throughout its lifecycle.

Information shall be protected according to its classification, sensitivity, business value, legal obligations, and regulatory requirements.

Security controls shall prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, modification, destruction, or loss of organizational information.

All personnel shall handle organizational information in accordance with this policy and related security standards.

---

# 5. Data Classification

Organizational information shall be classified according to the Enterprise Information Classification Policy.

Classification levels include:

- Public
- Internal
- Confidential
- Restricted

Security controls shall increase according to the sensitivity and business value of the information.

Data owners shall review classifications periodically to ensure continued accuracy.

---

# 6. Data Ownership

Every organizational information asset shall have an assigned Data Owner.

Data Owners are responsible for:

- Classifying information.
- Approving access requests.
- Reviewing user access.
- Defining retention requirements.
- Ensuring appropriate protection controls.
- Supporting regulatory compliance.
- Approving data disposal.

---

# 7. Data Handling Requirements

All organizational information shall be handled according to its classification level.

Handling requirements include:

- Access only by authorized personnel
- Secure storage
- Secure transmission
- Secure processing
- Secure sharing
- Secure disposal
- Protection from unauthorized disclosure
- Protection against unauthorized modification

Personnel shall use only organization-approved systems for storing and processing business information.

Sensitive information shall never be stored on unauthorized devices or services.

---

# 8. Data Access Control

Access to organizational information shall follow the Principle of Least Privilege and Need-to-Know.

Access controls shall include:

- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Strong password requirements
- Identity lifecycle management
- Privileged Access Management (PAM)
- Periodic access reviews
- Session monitoring where appropriate

Data Owners shall approve access to Confidential and Restricted information.

Access permissions shall be removed promptly when no longer required.

---

# 9. Data Encryption

Sensitive organizational information shall be encrypted during storage and transmission.

Encryption requirements include:

- AES-256 encryption for data at rest where supported
- TLS 1.2 or higher for data in transit
- Approved cryptographic algorithms
- Secure key management
- Certificate lifecycle management
- Encryption of backup media
- Encryption of removable media containing sensitive information

Encryption keys shall be protected against unauthorized access.

---

# 10. Data Sharing

Organizational information shall only be shared for legitimate business purposes.

Data sharing shall:

- Be approved by the Data Owner when required
- Use secure transmission methods
- Follow contractual and regulatory obligations
- Be limited to the minimum information necessary
- Be logged where appropriate

Information shared with third parties shall be protected through appropriate contractual, confidentiality, and security requirements.

---

# 11. Data Retention

Information shall be retained according to:

- Legal requirements
- Regulatory requirements
- Contractual obligations
- Business requirements
- Records retention schedules

Data Owners shall define retention periods for information under their responsibility.

Expired information shall be securely archived or destroyed according to approved procedures.

---

# 12. Secure Data Disposal

Information that is no longer required shall be disposed of securely.

Approved disposal methods include:

- Secure deletion
- Cryptographic erasure
- Physical destruction of storage media
- Certified media sanitization
- Secure shredding of paper records

Disposal activities shall comply with the organization's Media Sanitization Standard and applicable legal requirements.

---

# 13. Data Loss Prevention (DLP)

Apex Technologies shall implement Data Loss Prevention (DLP) controls to reduce the risk of unauthorized disclosure, transmission, or loss of sensitive information.

DLP controls shall monitor:

- Email communications
- Cloud storage
- File transfers
- Web uploads
- USB storage devices
- Collaboration platforms
- Endpoint devices
- Network traffic

DLP policies shall:

- Detect sensitive information
- Block unauthorized transmission
- Alert security personnel
- Log security events
- Support incident investigations

Security teams shall periodically review DLP rules to ensure effectiveness.

---

# 14. Backup and Recovery

Critical organizational information shall be protected through secure backup and recovery processes.

Backup requirements include:

- Scheduled backups
- Encrypted backup storage
- Offline backups where appropriate
- Immutable backups for critical systems
- Periodic recovery testing
- Secure backup retention
- Backup integrity verification

Recovery procedures shall support established Recovery Time Objectives (RTO) and Recovery Point Objectives (RPO).

---

# 15. Privacy Requirements

Personal information shall be processed in accordance with applicable privacy laws, regulations, contractual obligations, and organizational policies.

Privacy requirements include:

- Lawful collection of personal information
- Purpose limitation
- Data minimization
- Accuracy of personal information
- Secure storage
- Access controls
- Secure disposal
- Privacy impact assessments where required

Personal information shall only be accessible to authorized personnel.

---

# 16. Roles and Responsibilities

## Executive Management

Executive Management shall:

- Approve the Enterprise Data Protection Program.
- Allocate appropriate resources.
- Review enterprise data protection risks.
- Support compliance initiatives.

## Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)

The CISO shall:

- Own this policy.
- Approve enterprise data protection standards.
- Monitor compliance.
- Report significant risks to Executive Management.
- Promote continual improvement.

## Data Owners

Data Owners shall:

- Classify organizational information.
- Approve access requests.
- Define retention requirements.
- Review user access.
- Ensure appropriate protection controls.

## Information Security Team

The Information Security Team shall:

- Monitor data protection controls.
- Investigate security incidents.
- Conduct risk assessments.
- Perform compliance reviews.
- Support DLP operations.
- Recommend security improvements.

## IT Operations

IT Operations shall:

- Implement technical security controls.
- Maintain encryption technologies.
- Support backup and recovery.
- Protect organizational infrastructure.
- Maintain system availability.

## Employees

Employees shall:

- Handle information according to its classification.
- Protect organizational information.
- Report suspected data breaches.
- Complete required security awareness training.
- Comply with this policy and supporting standards.

## Third-Party Service Providers

Third-party providers shall:

- Protect organizational information.
- Comply with contractual security requirements.
- Report security incidents promptly.
- Support compliance assessments when required.

# 17. Compliance

Compliance with this policy shall be verified through:

- Internal security audits
- External security audits
- Regulatory assessments
- Data protection reviews
- Privacy compliance assessments
- Data Loss Prevention (DLP) monitoring
- Access control reviews
- Backup and recovery testing
- Management reviews

Failure to comply with this policy may result in:

- Corrective action plans
- Increased monitoring
- Risk acceptance review
- Disciplinary action
- Contractual enforcement for third parties
- Legal or regulatory action where applicable

---

# 18. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy shall:

- Be formally documented
- Include business justification
- Include a documented risk assessment
- Identify compensating security controls
- Be approved by the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)
- Include an expiration date
- Be reviewed annually

Approved exceptions shall be maintained within the Enterprise Exception Register.

---

# 19. Continuous Improvement

The Enterprise Data Protection Program shall be continuously improved through:

- Internal audits
- External audits
- Security assessments
- Data protection reviews
- Privacy impact assessments
- Threat intelligence
- Lessons learned from security incidents
- Regulatory updates
- Technology modernization
- Industry best practices

Program effectiveness shall be reviewed annually by Executive Management and the Information Security Steering Committee.

# 22. Definitions

**Data Owner** – Individual responsible for the classification, protection, and management of organizational information.

**Personal Information** – Information that identifies or can reasonably identify an individual.

**Data Loss Prevention (DLP)** – Technologies and processes designed to detect and prevent unauthorized disclosure of sensitive information.

**Encryption** – The process of converting information into a secure format that can only be read using authorized cryptographic keys.

**Media Sanitization** – The process of permanently removing information from storage media to prevent recovery.
