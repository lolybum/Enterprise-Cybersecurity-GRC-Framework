# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Enterprise Business Continuity Policy is to establish governance requirements for maintaining critical business operations during and after disruptive events that could impact the organization's ability to deliver products, services, or essential business functions.

This policy establishes a standardized Business Continuity Management (BCM) framework that enables the organization to prepare for, respond to, recover from, and adapt to operational disruptions while protecting employees, customers, business partners, and organizational assets.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All employees.
- Contractors.
- Consultants.
- Temporary workers.
- Third-party vendors.
- Managed service providers.
- Business partners.
- All business units.
- Corporate offices.
- Cloud environments.
- On-premises environments.
- Hybrid environments.
- Critical business processes.
- Information systems.
- Applications.
- Data centers.
- Remote work environments.
- Customer-facing services.

This policy applies to all organizational functions responsible for supporting or delivering critical business services.

---

# 3. Objectives

The objectives of this policy are to:

- Maintain continuity of critical business operations.
- Minimize operational disruption.
- Protect employees and organizational assets.
- Establish standardized business continuity procedures.
- Support rapid recovery of business services.
- Reduce financial and operational impact.
- Improve organizational resilience.
- Support regulatory and contractual compliance.
- Integrate business continuity with enterprise risk management.

---

# 4. Policy Statement

The organization shall maintain a formal Enterprise Business Continuity Management (BCM) Program to ensure the continued delivery of critical business functions during and following disruptive events.

Business continuity planning shall be risk-based, regularly tested, documented, maintained, and integrated with Enterprise Risk Management, Disaster Recovery, Incident Management, Cybersecurity, and Crisis Management programs.

Business continuity plans shall be reviewed, updated, and approved at least annually or following significant organizational, operational, or technology changes.

---

# 5. Business Continuity Governance

The organization shall maintain a formal Business Continuity Management (BCM) governance structure to oversee the planning, implementation, maintenance, testing, and continuous improvement of enterprise business continuity capabilities.

Business continuity governance shall include:

- Executive sponsorship.
- Business Continuity Steering Committee.
- Defined governance roles and responsibilities.
- Business continuity policies and standards.
- Enterprise-wide coordination.
- Risk-based decision-making.
- Performance monitoring.
- Regulatory compliance oversight.
- Periodic management reviews.
- Continuous improvement initiatives.

Business continuity governance shall align with Enterprise Risk Management and Information Security governance.

---

# 6. Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

The organization shall perform Business Impact Analyses (BIAs) to identify and prioritize critical business functions and supporting resources.

Business Impact Analysis activities include:

- Identification of critical business processes.
- Business dependency analysis.
- Operational impact assessment.
- Financial impact assessment.
- Regulatory impact assessment.
- Customer impact assessment.
- Recovery priority determination.
- Recovery Time Objective (RTO) determination.
- Recovery Point Objective (RPO) determination.
- Documentation of BIA results.

Business Impact Analyses shall be reviewed at least annually and following significant organizational or technology changes.

---

# 7. Critical Business Functions

Critical business functions shall be identified, documented, and prioritized to support continuity planning.

Critical function identification shall include:

- Mission-essential processes.
- Customer-facing services.
- Financial operations.
- Regulatory reporting.
- Identity and access management services.
- Information technology services.
- Cybersecurity operations.
- Vendor-dependent services.
- Executive communications.
- Human Resources support functions.

Business units shall maintain documentation identifying their critical business functions and dependencies.

---

# 8. Recovery Strategies

The organization shall establish documented recovery strategies appropriate to the business impact and risk associated with each critical function.

Recovery strategies may include:

- Alternate work locations.
- Remote work capabilities.
- Cloud-based recovery.
- High availability architectures.
- Redundant infrastructure.
- Manual processing procedures.
- Third-party service arrangements.
- Backup personnel assignments.
- Alternate communication methods.
- Emergency procurement procedures.

Recovery strategies shall be evaluated periodically to ensure continued effectiveness.

---

# 9. Recovery Time Objectives (RTO)

Recovery Time Objectives (RTOs) shall be established for all critical business functions and supporting systems.

RTO planning shall include:

- Business priority classification.
- Maximum acceptable downtime.
- Resource availability.
- Technology dependencies.
- Staffing requirements.
- Vendor dependencies.
- Recovery sequencing.
- Executive approval.
- Documentation updates.
- Periodic validation.

Approved RTOs shall be incorporated into Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery plans.

---

# 10. Recovery Point Objectives (RPO)

Recovery Point Objectives (RPOs) shall define the maximum acceptable amount of data loss for critical business processes.

RPO planning shall include:

- Data criticality assessment.
- Backup frequency.
- Transaction recovery requirements.
- Database recovery requirements.
- Cloud service recovery capabilities.
- Data replication requirements.
- Regulatory obligations.
- Business owner approval.
- Documentation updates.
- Periodic validation.

RPOs shall be aligned with enterprise backup and recovery capabilities.

---

# 11. Crisis Management

The organization shall maintain a formal Crisis Management capability to coordinate executive decision-making and organizational response during disruptive events.

Crisis management activities include:

- Crisis declaration procedures.
- Executive decision-making.
- Emergency response coordination.
- Incident escalation.
- Stakeholder communications.
- Regulatory notifications.
- Customer communications.
- Media coordination.
- Recovery oversight.
- Post-incident review.

Crisis Management activities shall integrate with the Enterprise Incident Management and Disaster Recovery Programs.

---

# 12. Business Continuity Plans

Each business unit shall maintain documented Business Continuity Plans (BCPs) appropriate to its critical business functions.

Business Continuity Plans shall include:

- Critical business functions.
- Recovery procedures.
- Recovery priorities.
- Alternate work locations.
- Manual workarounds.
- Technology dependencies.
- Vendor dependencies.
- Resource requirements.
- Emergency contact information.
- Recovery checklists.

Business Continuity Plans shall be reviewed and approved annually.

---

# 13. Communication Plans

The organization shall maintain documented communication procedures to support timely and accurate information sharing during disruptive events.

Communication planning shall include:

- Employee notification procedures.
- Executive communications.
- Customer communications.
- Vendor communications.
- Regulatory notifications.
- Emergency contact lists.
- Public relations coordination.
- Status reporting.
- Escalation procedures.
- Alternate communication methods.

Communication plans shall be periodically tested and updated.

---

# 14. Third-Party Business Continuity

Critical third-party service providers shall maintain business continuity capabilities appropriate to the services they provide.

Third-party continuity requirements include:

- Business continuity plans.
- Disaster recovery capabilities.
- Service Level Agreements (SLAs).
- Recovery objectives.
- Periodic testing.
- Vendor risk assessments.
- Regulatory compliance.
- Incident notification procedures.
- Audit rights.
- Continuous performance monitoring.

Third-party continuity capabilities shall align with the Enterprise Third-Party Security Policy.

---

# 15. Testing and Exercises

Business Continuity Plans shall be tested regularly to verify their effectiveness.

Testing activities include:

- Tabletop exercises.
- Functional exercises.
- Simulation exercises.
- Recovery validation.
- Communication testing.
- Alternate site testing.
- Vendor participation.
- Lessons learned documentation.
- Corrective action tracking.
- Executive review.

Testing results shall be documented and used to improve business continuity capabilities.

---

# 16. Roles and Responsibilities

## Executive Management

Executive Management shall:

- Approve the Enterprise Business Continuity Program.
- Allocate appropriate resources.
- Review enterprise resilience risks.
- Support continuous improvement initiatives.

## Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)

The CISO shall:

- Own this policy.
- Oversee enterprise business continuity governance.
- Review business continuity metrics.
- Coordinate with Crisis Management and Disaster Recovery teams.
- Report significant resilience risks to Executive Management.

## Business Continuity Manager

The Business Continuity Manager shall:

- Develop and maintain Business Continuity Plans.
- Coordinate Business Impact Analyses.
- Schedule continuity testing.
- Track corrective actions.
- Maintain continuity documentation.
- Coordinate enterprise continuity activities.

## Information Security Team

The Information Security Team shall:

- Support business continuity planning.
- Monitor cyber resilience risks.
- Participate in continuity exercises.
- Review continuity security controls.
- Recommend security improvements.

## Business Unit Managers

Business Unit Managers shall:

- Identify critical business functions.
- Maintain Business Continuity Plans.
- Participate in Business Impact Analyses.
- Support continuity testing.
- Validate recovery procedures.

## Employees

Employees shall:

- Understand their business continuity responsibilities.
- Participate in required training and exercises.
- Follow approved continuity procedures.
- Report incidents that may affect business continuity.
- Support recovery activities as directed.

Exceptions to this policy shall:

# 22. Definitions

**Business Continuity Management (BCM)** – A management framework that prepares an organization to continue delivering critical products and services during and after disruptive events.

**Business Continuity Plan (BCP)** – A documented set of procedures for maintaining or restoring critical business operations following a disruption.

**Business Impact Analysis (BIA)** – A process used to identify critical business functions, assess disruption impacts, and determine recovery priorities.

**Recovery Time Objective (RTO)** – The maximum acceptable time required to restore a business function or system following an interruption.

**Recovery Point Objective (RPO)** – The maximum acceptable amount of data loss measured in time before an interruption.

**Crisis Management** – The coordinated management of significant events that threaten the organization's operations, reputation, people, or assets.
